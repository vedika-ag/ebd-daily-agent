# EBD Daily: Website Template

Fill this exactly. Plain text only: no emojis, no bold, no italics, no links, no markdown headers in the output.

Title: {{Headline built from the 2-3 biggest stories, joined with commas and "&". Example: "UPI MDR Deferred Until After Diwali as Transactions Hit a Record, DailyObjects Targets ₹400 Cr Revenue & Smartphone Sales Set to Fall 12% This Festive Season"}}

Excerpt: {{One to two sentences summarising the whole page: the 2-3 biggest stories, with their key numbers.}}

Description:

{{Item 1 headline: plain sentence-style headline, no emoji}}
{{2-4 sentences of summary with the key data. No commentary.}} Source: {{Publication, Publication, Publication}}

{{Item 2 headline}}
{{Summary.}} Source: {{Publications}}

(repeat for every item, one blank line between items. Biggest or most useful story first.)

---

Rules for this template:
- Item headline sits on its own line, summary paragraph on the next line, with "Source: ..." at the end of the same paragraph.
- Sources are comma-separated, full publication names (Economic Times, ET Retail, Moneycontrol, Inc42, Entrackr, The Hindu BusinessLine). Include the original publication first, then any other outlets whose coverage you used.
- Repeat stories already published in an earlier daily are dropped or folded into the Description only if there is a genuinely new development. Tell Veddika outside the template.
- On the Radar items (upcoming dates, sales, deadlines) are written as items in the Description in the same format, placed last. Each one is a headline line plus a summary and a Source.

Reference example (an approved page):

Title: UPI MDR Deferred Until After Diwali as Transactions Hit a Record, DailyObjects Targets ₹400 Cr Revenue & Smartphone Sales Set to Fall 12% This Festive Season

Excerpt: NPCI defers UPI MDR on select merchant payments until after Diwali as September transactions hit a record 802 million a day, DailyObjects targets ₹400 crore in FY27 revenue after crossing ₹200 crore, and Counterpoint expects festive smartphone volumes to fall 12% as memory costs lift prices.

Description:

Spurred by festive shopping, UPI hits record transaction volume as MDR gets delayed until Diwali
UPI averaged a record 802 million transactions a day in September, with an average daily value of ₹97,913 crore. NPCI deferred MDR on select P2M transactions to immediately after Diwali, on a date to be notified. The structure is unchanged: 0.4% on specified merchant transactions above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 for transactions above ₹75,000, with transactions up to ₹2,000 free. Source: Moneycontrol, Business Standard, The Tribune

Smartphone sales set to fall 12% this festive season as rising prices hit demand: Counterpoint
Counterpoint expects festive-season smartphone volumes to fall 12% YoY while market value grows 5%, as higher memory costs lift device prices. The forecast covers the festive window from October 8 to November 8. Source: Fortune India, Counterpoint Research, Lapaas Voice
