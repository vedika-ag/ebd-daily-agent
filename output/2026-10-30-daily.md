# EBD Daily: Friday, 30 October 2026

🧴 **CureSkin posts ₹133 Cr revenue and ₹35 Cr loss in FY26**
CureSkin's revenue from operations rose 27.9% to ₹133 crore in FY26, per its Registrar of Companies filings, while its net loss narrowed 48.5% to ₹35 crore from ₹68 crore in FY25. Product sales brought in ₹120.6 crore and services about ₹12.4 crore. Advertising and promotional spend fell 17.6% to ₹61 crore.
*Source: Entrackr, NewsBytes*

🐱 **Smylo in talks to raise ₹30 Cr to expand its cat food portfolio**
D2C cat food startup Smylo is in advanced talks to raise about ₹30 crore (around $3.2 million) in a mix of equity and debt, mostly equity, according to sources. Founded in 2024 by former Titan Capital executives Abhishek Agrawal and Kartikeya Gupta, the brand sells preservative-free wet cat food. It earlier raised ₹75 lakh on Shark Tank India at a ₹75 crore valuation.
*Source: Inc42, GlobalPETS*

🛒 **Walmart probes Flipkart employees over kickbacks exceeding ₹150 Cr**
Walmart has opened an internal probe after whistleblower complaints alleged that employees in Flipkart's smartphones and luggage and travel accessories divisions took more than ₹150 crore from vendors in exchange for preferential treatment on the platform. The inquiry is ongoing and no disciplinary action has been confirmed. Reports describe it as the third financial misconduct probe at Flipkart in under three years.
*Source: Moneycontrol, Business Today, Storyboard18, Upstox*

⚖️ **Counting backwards: the prior-price rule and discount claims in Indian ecommerce**
The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026, notified on September 9 and in force from January 1, 2027, require any reduced price to be shown alongside a "prior price": the lowest price of that product in the 30 days before the price cut was announced. The rule targets raising prices just before a sale to inflate the apparent discount, but does not cap what sellers can charge. The column says the rule can curb misleading offers but needs clearer drafting.
*Source: Bar & Bench, The Policy Edge*

Indian D2C brands are moving from heavy festive discounting towards product quality and repeat-customer retention, as rising customer acquisition costs make deep discounts expensive to sustain.

🥗 **KazeLiving by KSKT expands into omni-channel retail**
KSKT Agromart, which runs the healthy food platform Kaze Living, opened its first Kaze Living Experience Centre at Starling Mall in Noida on October 24. It is the first of three planned centres, each pairing a food market with a café and doubling as a dark store for one-hour delivery. Hindustan Media Ventures has agreed to invest up to ₹9.34 crore in the company, whose turnover rose to ₹12.99 crore in FY25 from ₹5.45 crore.
*Source: Mint, KSKT Agromart, Whalesbook*

💍 **Karwa Chauth shopping spreads beyond the North as D2C sales surge 64%**
Sales on GoKwik's network of over 15,000 D2C brands rose 64% year on year in the six days before Karwa Chauth. Demand for Karwa Chauth-labelled products rose 3.4 times in central India, 2.7 times in western India and 2.3 times in eastern and northeastern India, but only about 3% in the south. Prepaid orders made up 60% of these purchases, and coupon usage fell to 47.4% from 51.9%.
*Source: Economic Times Retail, Passionate in Marketing, Business News Week*

📦 **Nasscom asks for new GST delivery rules to be pushed to January 2027**
Nasscom has asked the Finance Ministry to defer the revised GST rules for ecommerce delivery services, notified on October 27 and due to take effect on November 1, to January 1, 2027. The rules reportedly move specified delivery services supplied through ecommerce platforms to 5% GST without input tax credit, from 18%, and cover deliveries over any distance except postal and courier. Nasscom says companies need time to change ERP, invoicing and GST compliance systems.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard, MediaNama*

🚚 **Shadowfax's Q2 profit jumps over 5x to ₹68 Cr**
Shadowfax's revenue from operations rose 56.6% to ₹1,538 crore in Q2 FY27, while net profit rose to ₹68 crore from ₹13 crore a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 5.0%. It was the sixth straight quarter of 55% or higher revenue growth.
*Source: Inc42, Free Press Journal, Kotak Neo*

💊 **Ace Blend raises $9.9 Mn led by 3one4 Capital**
Nutrition brand Ace Blend raised about $9.9 million (around ₹95 crore) in a Series A2 round led by 3one4 Capital, with Fireside Ventures also investing. About 50% of its sales come from its own website and about 45% from quick commerce. It plans to add six quick commerce cities by year-end and targets ₹250 to 300 crore in annual recurring revenue within 12 to 18 months.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42, Exchange4media*
