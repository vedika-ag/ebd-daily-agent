🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **Flipkart Group CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy is leaving after 11 years.** Until a successor is named "in the coming weeks", Chief Product and Technology Officer Balaji Thiagarajan and the board will run the company *(Entrackr)*. Three sources told Inc42 that Walmart asked him to step down. They say a probe into alleged seller kickbacks, paid for better visibility and sourcing, has widened beyond phones and luggage to Myntra and Cleartrip. They also cite the delayed IPO and profitability targets. Sources expect more senior exits. Flipkart hasn't given a reason. *(Inc42)*
- **Amazon says its cumulative exports from India have reached $27 bn through Global Selling.** It added $7 bn this year, the same as last year, and exporters on the programme are up 20% to 2.4 lakh. By state, Delhi has 36,000+ exporters, Rajasthan 32,500+, UP 29,000+ and **Gujarat 28,000+**. The fastest-growing categories (CAGR) are health & personal care (45%), grocery (44%), beauty (42%) and toys (35%). Amazon's target is $80 bn by 2030. *(YourStory, ET Retail)*
- **Two D2C financials:** Marico bought 60% of the nutrition brand **Cosmix** in February for ₹375 Cr. Its FY26 revenue nearly doubled to **₹92 Cr** (from ₹47 Cr) and profit doubled to **₹12.5 Cr**, with a 19% EBITDA margin. Ads were its largest cost, at about 35% of spend. **Libas**, the ethnic wear brand, grew revenue 19% to **₹726 Cr**. Its loss narrowed slightly to ₹15.2 Cr, its material costs rose 34.6%, and its ad spend rose 17.9% to ₹66 Cr. *(Entrackr)*
- **FSSAI is tightening enforcement on packaged water.** It has written to all state food safety commissioners asking for regular inspections, risk-based sampling and action on non-compliance, all logged in FoSCoS. This follows its prosecution sanction against Bisleri over a sample found unsafe. Bisleri says its water is safe. FSSAI's CEO said the crackdown is "not meant to spread panic". *(ET Retail)*
- **UPI shake-up: PhonePe has revived its IPO and Navi has passed 5% share.** According to sources, PhonePe is targeting a March 2027 listing at a **$12–15 bn** valuation, with an offer for sale of up to ₹12,000 Cr, and plans to file papers by December-end. It expects clarity on UPI MDR and the 30% market-share cap by then *(Inc42)*. NPCI data for October shows Navi's share rising to **5.04%** while PhonePe (45.34%), Google Pay (32.09%) and Paytm (7.96%) each slipped. UPI processed 25.47 bn transactions in the month. *(Inc42)*
