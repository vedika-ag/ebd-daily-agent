## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- **New GST rules for online sellers take effect tomorrow (1 Nov).**
  - **Delivery services:** Specified delivery services supplied through ecommerce platforms move to 5% GST without input tax credit, from 18%, under Notification 09/2026. I found no government reply to Nasscom's request to defer this to January 2027.
  - **Seller registration:** CBIC has notified a simpler GST registration route for small sellers. Those passing on up to ₹2.5 lakh of input tax credit a month can register in another state using a platform's warehouse there as their business address, with physical verification only in their home state. About 9.5 lakh sellers are expected to benefit.
  - *(TaxGuru, Inc42, MediaNama, Free Press Journal, NewsBytes)*
- **Bijnis rebrands as Zorit, raises ₹27 Cr for B2B quick commerce.** The round was led by Sparrow Capital, with Info Edge, WaterBridge Ventures and Better Capital also taking part. Zorit now stocks goods in local dark stores and delivers to neighbourhood retailers in as little as 60 minutes. It says it has reached ₹120 Cr in annualised sales. Its FY26 loss was ₹44.04 Cr. *(Inc42, Indian Startup News)*
