Title: Honasa's Q2 Profit Jumps 87% to ₹73 Cr, Maharashtra FDA Suspends 105 Food Licences Including Blinkit and Zepto Units & Nykaa Lifts Marketing Spend 24%

Excerpt: Mamaearth parent Honasa's Q2 profit jumps 87% to ₹73 crore on 36% revenue growth, Maharashtra's FDA suspends 105 food licences including Blinkit and Zepto-linked outlets, and Nykaa raises marketing spend 24% to ₹457 crore. Also: Cashfree's checkout for AI agents, MakeMyTrip's Quick Ride deal, Sleepy Owl nearing breakeven, Dhanteras coin sales, Shiprocket's first profit and the week's $255 million in startup funding.

Description:

**Mamaearth parent Honasa's Q2 profit jumps 87% to ₹73 Cr as revenue grows 36%**
Honasa Consumer's net profit rose 87% to ₹73.25 Cr in Q2 FY27 from ₹39.23 Cr, while revenue from operations grew 36% to ₹731 Cr from ₹538 Cr. EBITDA nearly doubled to ₹93 Cr, lifting the margin to 12.7% from 8.9%, though gross margin eased to 69.9% from 70.5%. Ad spend rose 36% to ₹245 Cr, and underlying volume growth was 31.3%.
*Source: Entrackr, BW Disrupt, Storyboard18*

**Maharashtra FDA suspends licences of 105 food outlets, including Blinkit and Zepto-linked units**
Maharashtra's Food and Drug Administration suspended 105 food business licences, including outlets linked to Blinkit and Zepto, and ordered seven establishments to shut after a two-day pre-Diwali inspection drive on 4 and 5 November. Inspectors cited unhygienic manufacturing and storage, improper handling and packaging, no pest control and no temperature control. The FDA seized 39,485 kg of food worth ₹1.35 Cr from over 70 establishments.
*Source: Rediff, Outlook Business, DNA India, The Federal*

**Nykaa raises marketing spend 24% to ₹457 Cr as profit jumps 207%**
Nykaa's marketing and selling-and-distribution spend rose 24% year on year to ₹457 Cr in Q2 FY27, but fell as a share of revenue to 15.2% from 15.7%. Beauty accounted for ₹330 Cr, while fashion's spend fell to 22.7% of net sales value from 26.9%, which the company put down to more efficient performance marketing. The figure doesn't include branding, media and production costs, which Nykaa books separately.
*Source: Indian Television, exchange4media, Best Media Info*

**Cashfree launches Checkout for Agents to enable AI-led payments**
Cashfree's new Checkout for Agents lets AI agents read and work through its hosted checkout pages, such as the order total and payment options, while the customer still approves the final payment. Built on the open WebMCP standard, it covers all merchants on Cashfree's hosted checkout with no code changes, and Cashfree says it can cut the tokens an agent needs to complete checkout by more than 40%. AI agents make up less than 1% of checkout sessions across Cashfree merchants, but that traffic has grown about 4 to 5 times year on year.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard, BW Businessworld*

**MakeMyTrip enters shared mobility with a majority stake in Quick Ride**
MakeMyTrip has taken a majority stake in Bengaluru-based carpooling platform Quick Ride, for an undisclosed amount. Quick Ride's brand and ride-pooling business have moved into a new entity, Co Ride Mobility, which will sit in MakeMyTrip's ground transport arm alongside redBus and Savaari, with Quick Ride's promoters continuing to run it. Its EV taxi operations stay with the old entity, iDisha Info Labs.
*Source: ET Retail, Entrackr, Business Standard*

**Quick commerce apps hit delivery limits after a Dhanteras surge in gold and silver orders**
BigBasket reported a 146% jump in gold coin sales and a 234% rise in silver coin sales on Dhanteras. Heavy demand for gold and silver on Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Zepto led to delivery limits and surge pricing.
*Source: Business Today, Whalesbook*

**Sleepy Owl's FY26 revenue surges 86% to ₹82 Cr as it nears breakeven**
Coffee brand Sleepy Owl's revenue from operations rose 86% to about ₹82 Cr in FY26 from ₹44 Cr, while its net loss narrowed 17% to ₹1.76 Cr from ₹2.12 Cr. EBITDA margin was -1.8%. Ad spend was about ₹20 Cr, and coffee bean costs rose 84.3% to ₹43.5 Cr.
*Source: Entrackr, Scoopearth*

**Shiprocket swings to profit in Q2 FY27 as revenue rises 35% to ₹674 Cr**
Shiprocket posted a profit of ₹60 lakh in Q2 FY27, against a ₹20.3 Cr loss a year earlier, as revenue from operations rose 35% to ₹673.6 Cr. Its core business grew 25% to ₹461.5 Cr and emerging businesses grew 61% to ₹212.2 Cr. Revenue was up 14% from the June quarter.
*Source: Entrackr, NewsBytes*

**Indian startups raised $255 Mn this week**
Indian startups raised $255 Mn between 2 and 6 November, according to Inc42's weekly count. GIVA's $103 Mn round led the week, and D2C beauty brand Hyeglo Beauty raised $744K.
*Source: Inc42*

**Diwali gifting gets louder and more luxurious, from premium speakers to international getaways**
Diwali gifting this year is moving towards high-end items such as premium speakers and international holidays. Festive gift guides feature premium audio such as Apple's AirPods Max 2 and Sony's ULT Field 3 speaker, which was cut to about ₹12,990 from ₹24,990.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Today*
