## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 🛒 **India's online retail to cross $90 billion in 2026, with grocery overtaking mobiles: Redseer.** Redseer expects online retail to grow 22–24% this year, its fastest pace in five years, after about $38 billion of GMV in the first half (up roughly 25%). Grocery's share is projected to rise to 19% from 16%, while mobiles fall to about 16% from 19%.
