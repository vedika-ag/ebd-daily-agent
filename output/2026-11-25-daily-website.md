Title: 94% of India's Top Online Platforms Still Use Dark Patterns, Shein Pays $100 Mn to Settle FTC Claims & IKEA India Pushes Profitability to FY29

Excerpt: A LocalCircles study finds 94% of India's most-used online platforms still use dark patterns weeks before new rules take effect, Shein agrees to pay $100 million to settle US FTC claims over late deliveries and suppressed reviews, and IKEA India moves its profitability target to FY29 as revenue rises 14% to ₹2,100 crore.

Description:

**94% of India's most-used online platforms still use dark patterns: LocalCircles**
A LocalCircles study of 354 platforms across 45 sectors found that 334 (94%) use at least one dark pattern. Of 31 companies that told the CCPA they had none, 23 still use them, and 102 of 108 platforms run by listed companies do too. In 37 sectors, including online grocery and food delivery, no platform was compliant. The new rules against dark patterns take effect on 1 January.
*Source: Inc42, YourStory*

**Shein to pay $100 Mn to settle US FTC claims over late deliveries and suppressed reviews**
The FTC said Shein will pay $100 Mn to settle allegations that it advertised fast shipping while orders arrived days, weeks or months late, and that it suppressed negative reviews. The proposed order bars Shein from misrepresenting delivery times or suppressing reviews and needs court approval. Shein denied liability.
*Source: Reuters, CNBC*

**IKEA India pushes its profitability target to FY29 as revenue grows 14%**
IKEA India's revenue rose 14% to ₹2,100 Cr in FY26, which ended in August, but the company is still loss-making. It has moved its profitability target to FY29 from FY28. Its EBITDA loss narrowed to 29% of revenue from 33%, and it did not disclose its net loss. It plans 30 stores by 2030, many in smaller formats, and aims to raise local sourcing from 30% to 50%. Online sales made up 26% of revenue.
*Source: Mint, ET Retail, BW Businessworld*

**Shadowfax to buy WeFast India assets and Borzo IP for ₹23.3 Cr**
Shadowfax will pay $2.5 Mn (about ₹23.3 Cr) for Borzo's technology and IP in India, a licence to the Borzo trademark, and selected WeFast India assets, to enter on-demand intra-city deliveries for SMEs and person-to-person (C2C) customers. WeFast has over 32,000 delivery partners, handles more than 1.2 Cr deliveries a year and had about ₹200 Cr in adjusted revenue in CY2025. The deal is expected to close by 31 March 2027.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42*

**FirstCry doubles FC Qwik shipments to 2.5 lakh in September**
FirstCry's FC Qwik fast-delivery service shipped about 2.5 lakh orders in September, double June's 1.25 lakh, and expanded from 12 to 16 cities. Its logistics arm, RocketBees, handles over 60% of FirstCry's online shipments, cutting return-to-origin shipments by 50% and delivery complaints by 60%. Parent Brainbees grew Q2 FY27 revenue 14.1% to ₹2,396 Cr, and its net loss narrowed 75.1% to ₹12.6 Cr.
*Source: ET Retail*

**Amazon signs a reported $7.5 Bn deal for Decart's AI chip software and team**
Amazon has agreed to acquire Decart's Optimization Stack technology, which helps AI models run efficiently on different chips, and to hire its three co-founders and engineering team. Bloomberg puts the price at about $7.5 Bn; Amazon didn't disclose terms. The software will go to Annapurna Labs to improve Amazon's Trainium and Inferentia chips against Nvidia.
*Source: TechCrunch, GeekWire, Yahoo Finance*

**90% of Indian consumers prefer science-backed skincare: EVERQ–1Lattice report**
A study by Puresta's EVERQ and 1Lattice found that 90% of respondents prefer clinical evidence to brand storytelling when choosing skincare, and 93% say clinical evidence matters. Only 2% had visited a dermatologist, and 75% felt most skincare and haircare brands were similar.
*Source: Indian Television, Storyboard18, MxMIndia*

**Dairy Day bets on a sundae in a can to win quick commerce shoppers**
Dairy Day's quick commerce-only brand, Ob & Gob, sells a ready-to-eat sundae in a transparent, can-style pack. Dairy Day has said its quick commerce business more than doubled in a year and makes up close to 7% of its revenue.
*Source: afaqs!, The Tribune*

**JPMorgan sees 75% upside in Swiggy as its profitability strategy takes centre stage**
JPMorgan sees about 75% upside in Swiggy shares, with the company's profitability plans now the focus.
*Source: NDTV Profit*

**Lawvek raises $550K to help companies recover money owed by vendors**
AI-powered vendor recovery platform Lawvek raised $550K in a pre-seed round led by Neon Fund, with Inzas Ventures participating. Its AI agents check vendor contracts against invoices and usage records to find overcharges, missed rebates and unclaimed service credits, then file the claims.
*Source: Entrackr*

**How India's changing festive shopping habits are giving brands new stories to tell**
An exchange4media feature looks at how Indian festive shopping habits are changing and how brands are reflecting that in their festive storytelling.
*Source: exchange4media*
