# EBD Daily — Thursday, 26 November 2026

💪 **Marico-owned Cosmix doubles revenue and profit in FY26**
Cosmix is a digital-first, plant-based nutrition brand, and Marico bought 60% of it in February 2026 for ₹375 Cr. Its FY26 revenue from operations rose 95.7% to ₹92 Cr from ₹47 Cr, and net profit doubled to ₹12.5 Cr from ₹6 Cr. Advertising was its largest cost at about 35% of spend, up 85.7% to ₹26 Cr. EBITDA margin was 19%.
*Source: Entrackr*

🛒 **Flipkart CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy exits after 11 years**
Flipkart says it will name Krishnamurthy's successor in the coming weeks. Until then, Chief Product and Technology Officer Balaji Thiagarajan and the board will lead the company. Three sources told Inc42 that Walmart asked him to step down. They link this to a widening probe into alleged seller kickbacks, which now covers Myntra and Cleartrip, as well as to the delayed IPO and profitability targets. Flipkart's statement gave no reason.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42*

🌍 **Amazon's ecommerce exports from India reach $27 Bn as exporters rise 20% in a year**
Cumulative exports through Amazon Global Selling grew by $7 Bn this year, from $20 Bn in October 2025. Registered exporters rose 20% to 2.4 lakh. Delhi has the most exporters at over 36,000, ahead of Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat (over 28,000) and Maharashtra. Health and personal care, grocery, beauty and toys grew fastest. Amazon is targeting $80 Bn by 2030.
*Source: YourStory*

👗 **Libas' revenue grows 19% to ₹726 Cr in FY26; losses narrow marginally**
The ethnic wear brand's revenue from operations rose 19% to ₹726 Cr, while total expenses rose 19.6% to ₹742.8 Cr. Material costs grew 34.6% to ₹426.7 Cr and ad spend grew 17.9% to ₹66 Cr. Its net loss narrowed to ₹15.2 Cr from ₹16.5 Cr.
*Source: Entrackr*

💳 **PayU India turns adjusted EBITDA profitable in H1 FY27**
PayU posted an adjusted EBITDA profit of $12 Mn, against a $1 Mn loss a year earlier. Revenue fell 3.3% to $384 Mn in dollar terms but grew 6% in local currency, excluding acquisitions. Credit revenue rose 24% to $119 Mn, while payments revenue fell 12% to $265 Mn as PayU exited unprofitable merchant accounts.
*Source: Inc42, Entrackr*

💍 **Bootstrapped with ₹25 lakh, PRAO says it is at a ₹36 Cr run-rate in fast-fashion jewellery**
In a press release, PRAO says it started with ₹25 lakh and has reached a ₹36 Cr revenue run-rate without outside funding. It calls itself India's largest fast-fashion jewellery brand.
*Source: Business Standard, The Wire*

🛵 **Blinkit looks to smaller cities for its next growth phase**
Blinkit is pushing beyond the metros for its next phase of growth. Blinkit aims to reach about 3,500 stores by March 2027 and 5,000 by March 2028. CEO Albinder Dhindsa has said new stores in smaller cities reach around 1,000 orders a day about as quickly as new stores in metros.
*Source: The Morning Context, Business Today*

📲 **Navi crosses 5% UPI market share as PhonePe and Google Pay slip in October**
Navi's share of UPI transactions rose to 5.04% in October from 4.76%, on 1.27 Bn transactions. PhonePe's share fell to 45.34%, Google Pay's to 32.09% and Paytm's to 7.96%. By value, Navi processed ₹62,492 Cr, overtaking CRED (₹62,102 Cr) as the fourth-largest UPI app. UPI processed 25.47 Bn transactions in the month.
*Source: Inc42*

🥐 **Bikaji Foods enters premium bakery with European-inspired brand Mahè**
Mahè launches under Bikaji's new Bikaji Bakes platform and was developed with master baker T K Khaleel. A manufacturing facility in Karnataka will support it. Its range covers viennoiseries, artisan breads, savouries, cakes and "naked" base products for foodservice, and it will serve both retail and foodservice customers.
*Source: ET Retail*

🧥 **Shein's slow India start shows the limits of Reliance's retail reach**
Shein returned to India in early 2025 through Reliance Retail, after being banned in 2020. Bloomberg reports that the relaunch has been slow to take off.
*Source: Bloomberg*

📊 **Prosus' India ecosystem crosses 500 Mn users as food delivery spending weighs on profit**
Prosus reported ecosystem revenue of $5.7 Bn for the six months to September, up 59%. Adjusted EBITDA fell to $492 Mn from $530 Mn, after $344 Mn of investment in food delivery businesses iFood and Just Eat Takeaway. Its India ecosystem now has 500 Mn annual users and handles 450 Mn orders a month. Its India holdings include Swiggy, Meesho, ixigo and Rapido.
*Source: Entrackr*

🌿 **Nikhil Kamath backs indē wild as the beauty brand pushes global expansion**
Kamath invested through his platform WTF as part of indē wild's wider funding round, finalised in October 2026. He will also be associated with the brand's Moonlight Growth range. Founded in 2021, indē wild sells at Sephora in the UK and US across 197 stores. The company says its Champi Hair Oil is Nykaa's top-selling hair oil.
*Source: Storyboard18*

🤖 **Amazon and Meta reportedly in talks to lift the block on AI shopping agent Muse**
Amazon is reported to be in talks with Meta to let Muse, Meta's AI shopping agent, back onto Amazon.com. Amazon has blocked Muse since 20 September, saying Meta gave no notice and that Muse did not identify itself as an agent. Meta says Muse cannot see users' passwords or payment methods.
*Source: TechCrunch, Axios, Adweek*

🍊 **Coca-Cola brings back Gold Spot in cans, quick commerce first**
Coca-Cola India is relaunching the orange drink as a low-sugar beverage in cans, selling first through quick commerce. Gold Spot came to Coca-Cola in 1993 with Parle's aerated drinks business, along with Thums Up, Limca and Maaza. It was phased out in the early 2000s to avoid competing with Fanta.
*Source: ET Retail*

☕ **Nothing Before Coffee and Wellbeing Nutrition launch protein-infused Cloud Series**
The range has four iced lattes with 14g or 28g of whey protein per serving, supplied by Wellbeing Nutrition. It will be sold across NBC's 112+ stores in more than 45 cities, with Surat, Ahmedabad and Vadodara among the launch markets. NBC says it grew 1.5 times in FY26.
*Source: ET Retail*

🪔 **Quick commerce sees 44 Mn orders during Dhanteras-Diwali, GMV touches ₹2,500 Cr**
Redseer tracked seven platforms. Dhanteras was the biggest day, with GMV of ₹850–900 Cr against ₹490 Cr in 2025. Orders grew 1.6–1.9 times year on year while order values stayed flat. Steel utensils sold about 16 times normal-day levels on Dhanteras, and men's ethnic wear 15 times on Diwali. UP and East India grew fastest.
*Source: ET Retail*

🏦 **PhonePe revives IPO, targets listing by March 2027 at $12–15 Bn valuation**
According to merchant bankers and sources, PhonePe plans an offer for sale of ₹12,000–14,000 Cr and will file updated papers soon. Inc42 reports a filing by December-end. Revenue is growing around 40% year on year, excluding MDR and incentives, and PhonePe says it turned PAT-positive at group level in H1. It expects clarity on UPI MDR and the 30% market-share cap by December-end.
*Source: Inc42, YourStory*

💧 **FSSAI tells states to step up sampling and enforcement on packaged water**
FSSAI has written to state food safety commissioners asking for regular inspections, risk-based sampling and action on non-compliance in packaged drinking and mineral water, with results logged in FoSCoS. This follows FSSAI's prosecution sanction against Bisleri over a sample declared unsafe. Bisleri says its water is safe.
*Source: ET Retail*
