## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 💸 **Festive season is getting more expensive for ecommerce sellers.** Amazon has raised closing fees (₹1 more per item up to ₹500, ₹3 above that) and moved to a cancellation charge of up to 10% of order value. Flipkart now charges ₹30 for a late dispatch, ₹60 for a cancellation and ₹90 for a delayed-then-cancelled order, and is weighing a revision to fulfilment fees. Delhivery has added a ₹4 surcharge per express shipment and ₹2 per surface shipment for D2C brands. *Source: Inc42, The Week*
- 🎥 **Live2.ai raises ₹130 crore for shoppable video and live commerce.** The pre-Series B round, led by IvyCap Ventures, includes a secondary portion for early shareholders. The company, which builds video commerce tools for brand websites, says revenue has quadrupled in a year, with about 90% coming from outside India. *Source: YourStory, CIOL, Indian Startup Times*
