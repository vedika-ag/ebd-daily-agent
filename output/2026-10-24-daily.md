# EBD Daily — Saturday, 24 October 2026

🏷️ **Meesho's branded push reaches deeper into small-town India as demand grows**
Meesho Mall, the platform's branded storefront, grew net merchandise value 93% year-on-year in the June quarter. Mall orders more than tripled on the first day of this year's festive sale, and the number of Mall customers from Tier 3 and smaller markets nearly tripled. Meesho's total Day 1 orders rose about 75% year-on-year.
*Source: Business Standard, CIOL*

🛒 **Instacart in talks for a sale, holds early talks with DoorDash and Uber**
Instacart is exploring a sale and has held preliminary talks with Uber and DoorDash, with an investment bank advising it, according to The Information; there is no guarantee of a deal. The company was worth about $12 billion at Thursday's close, and a typical 30-40% premium would value it at roughly $16-17 billion. Instacart shares closed about 9% higher on Friday, while Uber fell around 3% and DoorDash 4%.
*Source: Reuters, The Information, US News*

🍞 **CCPA fines Britannia ₹1 lakh for misleading "100% Whole Wheat Bread" claim**
The Central Consumer Protection Authority found the bread contained 62% whole wheat flour, with the remaining 38% made up of other ingredients. It rejected Britannia's argument that "100%" referred only to the wheat component, noting that "Whole Wheat Bread" requires a minimum of 75% whole wheat flour. Britannia must stop the claims across its packaging, website and social media and file a compliance report within 15 days; the CCPA fined Mrs. Bector's (English Oven) ₹1 lakh over a similar "100% Atta Bread" claim earlier.
*Source: Bar and Bench, Taxguru, LiveLaw*

🪔 **Quick commerce and food delivery platforms see demand spike during Navratri**
Swiggy saw higher demand for vrat (fasting) dishes between October 17 and 20, with sabudana dishes the most-ordered fasting food nationwide, and sweet orders up 36.4% year-on-year. Orders for dandiya sticks peaked around 6 pm as shoppers used quick commerce for last-minute festive buys, while Zepto's festive clothing orders rose 400%.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, Zee Business, Indian Retailer, Whalesbook*

📉 **Festive sales see shoppers log in but hold back on big-ticket purchases**
Redseer estimated India's online festive sales at $7-8 billion in gross merchandise value during the first leg, October 8 to 18. Datum Intelligence put sales for the opening four days at ₹29,800-30,900 crore, down 7-10% from ₹33,200 crore in the same period last year, with card spending down 12-15%. A LocalCircles survey estimates urban festive discretionary spending at ₹1.97 lakh crore, down 6% from 2025.
*Source: Inc42*

📦 **Owning inventory widens the gap between Blinkit and Instamart; Swiggy shares hit a 52-week low**
About 90-95% of what Blinkit sells is now its own inventory, its parent Eternal said, after it capped foreign shareholding to qualify as an Indian-owned company under FDI rules that bar foreign-owned marketplaces from holding stock. Instamart, which starts the same shift mid-quarter after Swiggy shareholders approved a 49.5% foreign ownership cap, posted an adjusted EBITDA loss of ₹895 crore in the September quarter against Blinkit's ₹184 crore profit. Swiggy shares fell to a 52-week low after its results, as Instamart's contribution margin slipped to −1.0% of gross order value from −0.2% and brokerages cut their targets.
*Source: MediaNama, Inc42*

💸 **D2C brands target 30% festive growth as acquisition costs rise 20% and baskets shrink**
D2C brands are aiming for about 30% festive growth even as customer acquisition costs climb about 20% and basket sizes get smaller. A separate estimate puts median acquisition cost for Indian D2C brands at about ₹620, up about 18% year-on-year, with festive marketing budgets up 15-30%.
*Source: ET Retail, Best Media Info*

🎬 **Reel deal: video is becoming a key way to shop online**
Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and Meesho are adding creator-led video and live-streaming features to shorten the path from discovery to checkout, with video commerce expected to contribute $25 billion to Indian ecommerce GMV by 2030. Meesho reports a 152% year-on-year rise in net merchandise value in its content-commerce business, and Amazon says 70% of its creator-led sales come from Tier 2 and Tier 3 locations.
*Source: ET Retail, Whalesbook*
