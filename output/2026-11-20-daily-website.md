Title: UPI MDR May Slip Past 2027 State Polls, Wedding Season Set for ₹7.5 Lakh Cr in Business & Urban Company Raises ₹2,500 Cr via QIP

Excerpt: The UPI MDR rollout may be pushed beyond the 2027 state elections, CAIT expects 52 lakh weddings to generate ₹7.5 lakh crore in business by 15 December, and Urban Company raised ₹2,500 crore through a QIP subscribed nearly six times. Flipkart's Big Billion Days timing cut Walmart International's Q3 growth by about 500 basis points, and quick commerce GMV hit ₹10,800 crore in October.

Description:

**UPI MDR rollout may be pushed beyond the 2027 state elections**
The rollout of the merchant discount rate (MDR) on UPI payments may be delayed until after the state elections in the first half of 2027, which cover Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Goa and Manipur. It was due on 15 October and was deferred until after Diwali, and NPCI has not announced a new date. The All India Consumer Products Distributors Federation has asked for the rollout to be suspended and for merchant-to-merchant payments to be exempted. Shares of Paytm, Pine Labs and MobiKwik fell after the report.
*Source: Inc42, Business Today*

**India's wedding season set to generate ₹7.5 lakh Cr in business from 52 lakh weddings: CAIT**
The Confederation of All India Traders estimates 52 lakh weddings between 21 November and 15 December, generating about ₹7.5 lakh Cr in business. Goods are expected to account for about 54% of spending, led by jewellery (₹1.2 lakh Cr) and apparel (₹90,000 Cr), followed by catering (₹75,000 Cr) and venues (₹60,000 Cr). Delhi-NCR alone is projected to see around 5 lakh weddings worth ₹2.5 lakh Cr.
*Source: ET Retail, The Tribune, The Statesman, Zee Business*

**Flipkart's Big Billion Days timing cuts Walmart International's Q3 growth**
Walmart said the timing of Flipkart's Big Billion Days sale reduced International net sales growth by about 500 basis points in Q3 FY27, and it expects the same amount to benefit Q4. International net sales rose 7.0% to $35.9 Bn, or 3.3% in constant currency. International advertising fell 15% and its ecommerce sales fell 2%, both because of the sale timing.
*Source: Inc42, Investing.com, Daily Excelsior*

**Urban Company raises ₹2,500 Cr through a QIP subscribed nearly six times**
Urban Company allotted about 16.03 crore shares at ₹156 each to 33 investors. The price was about 3% below the ₹161.03 floor price, and bids topped ₹15,000 Cr. Mutual funds took over half the allotment. The issue ran from 16 to 19 November, and proceeds will go towards its InstaHelp supply network, marketing, technology and possible acquisitions.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard, Business Today, Angel One*

**Razorpay to file its updated draft papers as early as next week, eyes IPO by February 2027**
Razorpay may publicly file its updated draft red herring prospectus (UDRHP) as early as next week and is targeting a listing around February 2027. SEBI has reportedly cleared the ₹6,000 Cr IPO, which could value the company at about $5 Bn, below its $7.5 Bn valuation in 2021. Razorpay filed its draft papers through the confidential route on 12 June 2026.
*Source: Inc42, Angel One*

**Quick commerce profitability now matches kiranas as platforms lose their margin edge for consumer companies**
Consumer companies say profit margins on quick commerce have fallen to levels similar to kiranas and general trade. Shrikant Kanhere, managing director and CEO of AWL Agri Business, said profits are now "more or less similar" across general trade and alternative channels. He attributed this to spending on promotions, schemes and search placement on the platforms.
*Source: Economic Times*

**Quick commerce GMV hits ₹10,800 Cr in October, up 8% from September**
Quick commerce GMV reached ₹10,800 Cr in October, about 13.5% of the ₹80,200 Cr in digital commerce GMV, according to 1DigitalStack. Amazon and Flipkart together recorded ₹34,000 Cr (42.4%). Blinkit, Swiggy and Zepto sold ₹110 Cr of baby food in October, against ₹23.5 Cr on Amazon.
*Source: NewKerala*

**Quick commerce ad rates up 30–40% this festive season**
Datum Intelligence estimates that ad rates on quick commerce platforms rose 30–40% this festive season. It projects quick commerce festive ad spend at about ₹2,200 Cr, up roughly 50% on last year, within total festive ad spend of ₹60,000–62,000 Cr.
*Source: Storyboard18, Lapaas Voice*

**India's retail sales rise 8% in September as festive demand builds**
Retail sales rose 8% year on year in September 2026, down from 10% in August, according to the Retailers Association of India's business survey. West India led at 10%, followed by South (9%), North (7%) and East (5%). Quick-service restaurants grew 11%, food and grocery and apparel 9% each, jewellery and sports goods 6%, and footwear 5%. Navratri fell in October this year, compared with September last year, which affects the comparison.
*Source: ET Retail, Indian Retailer, Fashion Value Chain*

**Walmart raises its full-year outlook for the third time after a solid quarter, fuelled by ecommerce**
Walmart's Q3 FY27 revenue rose 5.2% to $188.8 Bn. Global ecommerce sales grew 17%, US ecommerce 23% and advertising 25%. It now expects FY27 net sales growth of 4.9–5.2% in constant currency, up from 4–5%, and raised its adjusted EPS guidance to $2.84–2.89 from $2.80–2.87.
*Source: ET Retail, Yahoo Finance, Zacks*

**Amazon threatens to move delivery operations out of New York City over proposed delivery bill**
Amazon is stepping up opposition to New York City's Delivery Protection Act (Intro 518), which would require delivery workers to be hired as employees rather than through contracted delivery firms. Amazon says the bill could add up to $5 to each order and put more than 5,000 jobs at risk, and that it would look at moving delivery operations beyond city limits. The bill has 35 co-sponsors, a majority of the Council, and City Council Speaker Julie Menin called Amazon's campaign "a cynical ploy to trick New Yorkers."
*Source: Bloomberg, CBS New York, Fox Business, City & State New York*

**ABFRL expects luxury to make up 30–33% of revenue in 4–5 years, up from 20%**
Sathyajit Radhakrishnan, CEO of International Brands at Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail, said the luxury business is expected to contribute 30–33% of revenue in four to five years, up from 20% today. ABFRL's luxury segment grew 15% to ₹596 Cr in FY26 and 30% in Q1 FY27, helped by the ramp-up of its Galeries Lafayette store in Mumbai.
*Source: ET Retail, Storyboard18, Texspace Today*

**DCGpac's FY26 revenue rises 35% to ₹208 Cr; stays EBITDA positive**
B2B packaging company DCGpac's revenue from operations rose 34.6% to ₹208.5 Cr in FY26. It posted EBITDA of ₹60 lakh, a 0.28% margin. Its net loss was ₹3.15 Cr, which includes a deferred tax charge of ₹2.17 Cr.
*Source: Entrackr*

**NoBroker's home services business serves 2 lakh customers a month as festive transactions rise 30%**
NoBroker says its home services business now serves 2 lakh customers a month, and festive-season transactions rose 30%.
*Source: ET Retail*
