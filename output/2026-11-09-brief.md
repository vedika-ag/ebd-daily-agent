🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **Online festive sales were expected to rise about 25% to roughly $16 Bn (₹1.4 lakh crore) in the run-up to Diwali.** The Redseer estimate, reported by Bloomberg, compares with Redseer's ₹1.15 lakh crore forecast for last year's festive period. *This is a pre-Diwali projection; no final total is out yet.*
- **D2C brands' own websites got 27% more orders ahead of Diwali, and sales value rose 45%.** ClickPost analysed more than 8 crore orders across about 400 D2C brands. Hyperlocal delivery on brand websites reached 5% of orders, up from 2.6% last year. Kearney estimates D2C customer acquisition costs rose 15% to 20%, to about ₹600 to ₹650.
- **Quick commerce order volumes rose 75% year on year this festive season, the fastest of any channel.** Overall ecommerce orders grew 21% from 6 October to 4 November, and quick commerce GMV rose 38%, according to Unicommerce data on its Uniware platform. D2C brand websites saw a 30% rise in orders and 26% higher GMV. *Only one outlet reports these full-season figures; Unicommerce's first-week data showed quick commerce up 85%.*
