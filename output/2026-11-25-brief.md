🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **94% of India's most-used online platforms still use dark patterns (LocalCircles).** The study covered 354 platforms across 45 sectors. Of the 31 companies that told the CCPA their platforms had no dark patterns, 23 (74%) still use them, and 102 of 108 platforms run by listed companies do too. In 37 sectors, including online grocery and food delivery, no platform was found compliant. The new e-commerce rules on dark patterns take effect on **1 January**. *(Inc42, YourStory)*
- **Shadowfax is buying WeFast India's assets and Borzo's IP for about ₹23.3 Cr ($2.5 Mn)** to enter on-demand intra-city deliveries for SMEs and person-to-person (C2C) customers. WeFast has more than 32,000 delivery partners, handles over 1.2 Cr deliveries a year, and had about ₹200 Cr in adjusted revenue in CY2025. The deal is expected to close by 31 March 2027. *(Entrackr, Inc42)*
- **IKEA India's FY26 revenue grew 14% to ₹2,100 Cr, and it now targets profitability by FY29.** It plans 30 stores by 2030, many in smaller formats, with Jaipur, Lucknow and Chandigarh next. It also plans to raise local sourcing from 30% to 50% by FY30. Online sales were 26% of revenue, and same-store sales grew 4–5%. *(ET Retail)*
