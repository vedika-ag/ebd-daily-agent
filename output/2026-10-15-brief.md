## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 📊 **D2C share of ecommerce projected to reach 30% by 2030.** A report by Google, WPP Media and Elevation Capital projects D2C online sales growing 4.5x by 2030 into a $72 billion revenue pool, lifting D2C brands' share of ecommerce from 18% to 30%. The report is based on Kantar research with 1,500 respondents; 79% of challenger-brand buyers said they doubt a brand they can't find on Google Search. *Source: Storyboard18, Indian Television, MarketingMind*
- 🏭 **Wholesale inflation jumps to 11.39% in September.** WPI inflation rose from 9.92% in August on higher food, fuel and manufactured-goods prices. Manufactured products inflation climbed to 9.76% from 8.37%, and April–September cumulative inflation stood at 9.85%. *Source: Business Standard*
- 📦 **ShipMaxx360 spins out as a full-stack partner for ecommerce brands.** The logistics and commerce-enablement platform has separated from Losung360 into an independent company, targeting more than 10,000 ecommerce and D2C brands by 2027. *Source: The Tribune*
