Title: Festive Online Sales Rise 17% to ₹1.41 Lakh Crore, SoftBank Sells 1.65% of Meesho for ₹1,885 Cr & FirstCry Cuts Q2 Loss 75%

Excerpt: Online festive sales grew 17% to about ₹1.41 lakh crore, the slowest growth since 2023, as quick commerce raised its share to about 16%. SoftBank sold 1.65% of Meesho for ₹1,885 crore in a block deal, and FirstCry cut its Q2 FY27 loss 75% to ₹12.6 crore as revenue neared ₹2,400 crore.

Description:

**Festive online sales rise 17% to ₹1.41 lakh crore, the slowest growth since 2023**
Datum Intelligence estimates online festive sales at about ₹1.41 lakh crore over 34 sale days to 9 November, about ₹14,200 crore below its September forecast. Quick commerce orders rose 75%, brand websites grew 27% to 30% and marketplace orders grew 8%. Online smartphone volumes fell 25% to 33% as average prices rose 16% to 19%.
*Source: Moneycontrol, Business Standard, The Statesman*

**Quick commerce takes a bigger slice of a muted festive season**
Quick commerce made up about 16% of festive online sales, up from 12% a year earlier, according to Datum Intelligence data, and its festive GMV rose about 60% to roughly ₹22,528 crore. Amazon Now's orders rose 18 times over Diwali, Flipkart Minutes' rose 4 times and Swiggy Instamart's rose 162%. Dark stores reached about 7,153, up 34%.
*Source: Financial Express, Lapaas Voice, ET Now*

**SoftBank sells 1.65% of Meesho for ₹1,885 crore in a block deal**
SoftBank's SVF II Meerkat sold about 7.64 crore Meesho shares at ₹246.75 each on 13 November, cutting its holding from 6.84% to about 5.19%. The shares were sold at about a 4% discount to Friday's close. SoftBank had already sold 1.73% of the company for about ₹1,650 crore in September.
*Source: Entrackr, Kotak Neo, Inc42*

**FirstCry nears ₹2,400 crore revenue in Q2 FY27 as losses narrow 75%**
FirstCry parent Brainbees Solutions cut its Q2 FY27 net loss 75% to ₹12.6 crore, from ₹50.5 crore a year earlier. Revenue from operations rose 14.1% to ₹2,395.83 crore. Its India multichannel business grew 18.6%, its fastest in eight quarters, and consolidated adjusted EBITDA margin was 4.9%.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42, The Shillong Times*

**Delhivery signs MoUs with Tata Motors, Euler and Montra for up to 2,000 electric vans**
Delhivery plans to deploy 1,500 to 2,000 four-wheeler electric commercial vehicles across its intra-city network over 12 to 18 months. This is the first phase of a target of about 5,000 within three years. EVs currently make up 12% to 13% of its eligible intra-city fleet; the rollout targets routes under 200 km a day and starts in Delhi-NCR.
*Source: Business Standard, Daily Excelsior*

**Quick commerce ad rates jump 30% to 40% as brands turn selective**
Quick commerce ad rates rose 30% to 40% this festive season, and festive ad spend on the channel reached about ₹2,200 crore, up 50%, according to Datum Intelligence. Rising costs for sponsored listings and preferred placements have led brands such as Zydus Wellness and Milky Mist to spend more selectively.
*Source: Mint, Lapaas Voice, Exchange4media*

**Amazon Bazaar gains ground in value commerce as Meesho and Shopsy compete: Infisum**
Amazon Bazaar's GMV grew about fivefold year on year in 2026, according to an Infisum Modeling report, with most of its products priced under ₹300. It competes with Meesho, Flipkart's Shopsy and Reliance's Ajio Street. The report expects India's value commerce market to reach $65 to 70 billion by 2030, led by Tier 2 and smaller cities.
*Source: Storyboard18, Fluf*

**Beco bets on quick commerce and targets 25,000 general trade stores**
Home care brand Beco gets about 45% of its revenue from quick commerce and is listed in about 90% of relevant dark stores, co-founder Akshay Varma said. It is in about 5,000 general trade outlets across nine cities and aims to reach more than 25,000 within two to three years. It also sells through about 150 DMart stores and more than 250 Reliance Retail outlets.
*Source: Indian Retailer, D2C Insider*

**Diwali shopping expands beyond essentials as fashion and gifting gain ground**
Festive spending this Diwali went beyond sweets and puja items into appliances, kitchenware, personal care, fashion and gifting. Zepto's fashion demand doubled from last year, and orders for traditional gifting items such as envelopes rose fourfold, especially on Dhanteras.
*Source: Business Standard*

**Swiggy, Zepto and Eternal sit on the same board as the government sets up a gig worker welfare panel**
The Labour Ministry has set up a National Social Security Board for gig and platform workers with a three-year term, chaired by the Union Labour Minister. Eternal, Swiggy, Zepto, Urban Company and Rapido each get one seat, alongside worker unions, and the board will meet at least three times a year. Under the Code on Social Security, aggregators must contribute 1% to 2% of annual turnover to worker welfare, capped at 5% of their payouts to workers.
*Source: Mint, Business Standard, KNN India*

**Atomberg plans ₹100 crore R&D spend from its IPO proceeds**
Atomberg's planned IPO includes a ₹450 crore fresh issue, of which ₹100 crore is earmarked for R&D, ₹150 crore for brand and performance marketing and ₹90 crore for debt repayment. The company spent ₹86.79 crore on R&D in FY26, or 6.71% of revenue. Atomberg received SEBI approval for the IPO in October.
*Source: Indian Retailer, YourStory, Angel One*

**Ajinkya Rahane picks up a stake in D2C cookware brand Ember**
Former cricketer Ajinkya Rahane has invested an undisclosed amount in Bengaluru-based Ember Cookware. Ember was founded in 2024 by Siddharth Gadodia, Himanshi Tandon and Bret Recor and raised a $3.2 million seed round in September 2025. It plans to expand distribution across major retail channels and online.
*Source: Inc42, MediaBrief*

**D2C menswear brand QB Men raises pre-seed funding led by BeyondSeed**
Jaipur-based QB Men, a print-led menswear brand founded by Yash Bothra, raised an undisclosed pre-seed round led by BeyondSeed, with Rajkot Ventures and Prajay Advisors also taking part. The brand reports a 23.8% repeat purchase rate and targets 100% growth over the next 12 months. It plans to add chinos, trousers and kidswear.
*Source: Entrackr, D2C Insider, Indian Startup Times*

**D2C startups take about 30% of startup funding in October**
D2C startups raised about $200 million in October, nearly 30% of the $678 million raised by Indian startups that month, and accounted for about one in three funding deals. Boldfit's $42 million Series B was the largest D2C round.
*Source: Entrackr*

**D2C brands see 45% GMV growth over Diwali as shoppers buy bigger, pricier carts: ClickPost**
ClickPost's Great Indian Festive Report 2026 covers about 8 crore orders from nearly 400 D2C brands. It found festive GMV up 45% year on year, about 1.7 times the 27% growth in order volume, which the report attributes to higher-value carts. The report doesn't separate how much of the rise came from higher prices, bigger baskets or premium products.
*Source: Storyboard18, ClickPost, Medianews4u*

**Brands give festive outdoor ads a creative spin with interactive billboards**
Zepto, Philips, IKEA and Flipkart used interactive 3D installations and live product demos in their festive outdoor advertising this year. In one Zepto and Philips street activation, a steamer was aimed at an outfit on display, so the appliance's function became part of the ad.
*Source: Storyboard18*

**D2C brands turn to hybrid fulfilment models to speed up deliveries**
D2C brands are moving to hybrid fulfilment models to deliver faster.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine*

**Quick commerce advertising faces an accountability test as brands question media wastage**
Brands are questioning how much of their quick commerce ad spend is wasted and how results are measured.
*Source: Exchange4media*

**Over ₹10 lakh fine each on Flipkart and a coconut oil seller for a misleading ad**
Flipkart and a coconut oil seller have each been fined over ₹10 lakh for a misleading advertisement.
*Source: The New Indian Express, Fluf*

**Black Friday falls on 27 November, with Cyber Monday on 30 November**
Black Friday falls on 27 November this year, with Cyber Monday on 30 November. No major platform has confirmed its 2026 sale dates yet.
*Source: Zoutons*
