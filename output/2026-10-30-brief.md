## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- **Nasscom asks for new GST delivery rules to be pushed to January 2027.** The rules were notified on 27 Oct and take effect on 1 Nov. They reportedly move specified delivery services supplied through ecommerce platforms to 5% GST with no input tax credit, from 18%, and cover deliveries over any distance except postal and courier. Nasscom told the Finance Ministry on 28 Oct that companies need time to change ERP, invoicing and GST-compliance systems. No government response yet. *(Inc42, Business Standard, MediaNama)*
- **Shadowfax's Q2 profit up about 5x.** The logistics firm's September-quarter revenue rose 56.6% to ₹1,538 Cr, and net profit rose to ₹68 Cr from ₹13 Cr. Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 5.0%. It is the sixth straight quarter of 55%+ revenue growth. *(Inc42, Free Press Journal, Kotak Neo)*
- **Ace Blend raises $9.9 Mn (about ₹95 Cr), led by 3one4 Capital.** Fireside Ventures also invested in the nutrition D2C brand's Series A2 round. About 50% of its sales come from its own website and about 45% from quick commerce. It plans to expand quick commerce to Pune, Kolkata, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Ludhiana and Chennai by year-end, and is targeting ₹250–300 Cr ARR (annual recurring revenue) in 12–18 months. *(Entrackr, Inc42, Exchange4media)*
