# EBD Daily — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

🧻 **Pee Safe's FY26 revenue jumps 54% to ₹126 Cr; loss widens to ₹7.2 Cr**
Pee Safe's operating revenue rose 53.7% to ₹126 Cr in FY26 from ₹82 Cr, while its loss grew 80% to ₹7.2 Cr from ₹4 Cr. Advertising spend rose 76.3% to ₹46 Cr, about 34% of total costs, and total expenses rose 54.9% to ₹134 Cr. The hygiene brand spent ₹1.06 to earn each rupee of revenue.
*Source: Entrackr*

💍 **Silver jewellery brand Zavya raises ₹22.6 Cr in pre-Series A round**
Zavya raised ₹22.6 Cr ($2.5 Mn) from Daksham Growth Ventures and existing investor Orios Venture Partners, at a post-money valuation of about ₹191.5 Cr. The EBITDA-profitable 925 sterling silver brand, founded in 2021, will invest in D2C and quick commerce growth and open its first physical stores.
*Source: Entrackr*

👓 **Steadview Capital sells 0.73% of Lenskart for ₹921 Cr in a block deal**
Steadview Capital sold 1.27 Cr Lenskart shares at ₹725 each on 23 November, a 2.6% discount to the previous close, for ₹920.58 Cr. Société Générale and Goldman Sachs bought the shares. Lenskart closed 4% lower at ₹714.50, which values the company at about ₹1.24 lakh Cr, after a Q2 FY27 in which revenue grew 35.7% to ₹2,845 Cr and net profit more than doubled to ₹232 Cr.
*Source: Entrackr*

🍛 **Delhi restaurant chain Sita Ram pulls out of Zomato over a commission hike**
Sita Ram by Pran Nath Kohli has stopped taking orders on Zomato across all its Delhi outlets, citing a sudden increase in the commission the platform charges. The chain, with outlets in Paharganj, Laxmi Nagar, Krishna Nagar, Sadiq Nagar and Lajpat Nagar, remains available on other delivery platforms. It hasn't said whether the exit is temporary.
*Source: Storyboard18*

⚡ **Flipkart doubles its last-mile EV fleet to 42,000 vehicles**
Flipkart's electric delivery fleet has grown to 42,000 vehicles from just over 20,000 a year ago, led by quick commerce. Electric two-wheelers used for Flipkart Minutes make up about 77% of the fleet. It runs eight battery-swapping stations in Bengaluru, and a survey of 9,400+ delivery partners found that riders who had tried an EV were nearly four times as likely to switch. Flipkart targets a fully electric last-mile fleet by 2030.
*Source: Logistics Insider, Business Standard, Indian Television*

🚫 **FSSAI suspends licences of five nutraceutical makers**
FSSAI suspended the licences of Vedant Remedies, Lucid Healthcare, Ambico Ayurvedic Healthcare, Vitamedic Health Nutraceuticals and Adorreal Healthcare with immediate effect on 23 November, citing food safety, hygiene and regulatory violations. Vedant Remedies scored just 12% on compliance, with expired flavourings and a licence registered to premises that don't exist. The premises on Vitamedic's and Adorreal's licences were not operating.
*Source: The Hindu, Business Standard, ETV Bharat*

A guest column in afaqs! argues that brands should keep marketing to consumers across the whole lifecycle, not just at the point of acquisition.
*Source: afaqs!*

⠿ **ITC extends Braille packaging to Fiama, taking the total to 16 SKUs**
ITC's personal care business has extended Braille identifiers to Fiama shower gels, bringing the total to 16 SKUs. The packs also include Savlon Antiseptic Liquid, Savlon wet wipes and handwash pumps. Savlon introduced India's first Braille-enabled FMCG packaging in 2018.
*Source: News18, Business Standard*

🧩 **D2C brands expand into entirely new categories**
Bombay Shaving Company's parent, Visage Lines, has launched rE, a recovery and wellness brand selling massage guns, foam rollers and scalp massagers. That puts it into a market for at-home recovery devices that some reports put at over $400 Mn. Snitch has entered luggage, and Cult.fit is positioning its sneakers for everyday wear. These brands are using their existing customers to cross-sell into new verticals.
*Source: Inc42*

🏭 **Parag Milk Foods opens a 53,837 sq ft distribution hub in Bhiwandi**
The Bhiwandi hub stores about 1,100 tonnes, handles 240 tonnes a day and has space for 2,700 pallets, or about 24,000 cases a day. It will serve Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, the wider Mumbai region, Vasai-Virar, Palghar, Raigad and Panvel for the Gowardhan, Go and Avvatar brands. Parag plans to add technology-led warehouse operations progressively through FY28.
*Source: ET Retail*

🔐 **RBI proposes a "Stop Switch" to block digital payment fraud**
RBI's draft rules would let customers instantly block all outgoing debits, new payment mandates and new UPI app registrations, while incoming credits and existing standing instructions continue. It also proposes a voluntary "Trusted Person" who must approve high-value transfers within one hour. Feedback is open until 14 December 2026, and the rules would take effect on 1 July 2027.
*Source: Inc42*

🇪🇺 **India–EU FTA likely to be signed in December, says EU Ambassador**
The EU Ambassador said the India–EU free trade agreement is expected to be signed in December and could come into force by mid-2027, after the European Parliament reviews it. Negotiations concluded at the India–EU summit in January, and EU ministers are now considering approving the signing.
*Source: ET Retail*
