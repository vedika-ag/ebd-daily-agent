## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 📦 **Festive ecommerce orders up 31%, quick commerce up 85%: Unicommerce.** In the first week of festive sales (7–13 Oct), ecommerce order volumes rose 31% year-on-year and GMV 17%, based on 33 million order items. Quick commerce orders rose 85%, marketplaces 26% and brand websites 27%. Average order value fell 10%, Tier 3 towns made up 42% of orders, and FMCG volumes rose more than 190%. *Source: Business Standard, Indian Television, Free Press Journal*
