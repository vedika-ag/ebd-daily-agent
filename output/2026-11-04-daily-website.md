Title: GIVA Raises Over $100 Mn Led by Multiples, FMCG Brands Lift Festive Ad Spends 10-15% & FirstClub Crosses 3.5 Million Orders as It Enters Beauty

Excerpt: GIVA raises over $100 million in a Multiples-led round, FMCG brands lift festive ad budgets 10-15% as Nestlé India and Marico raise spends by over 40%, and FirstClub crosses 3.5 million orders as it launches beauty and personal care with 80+ brands. Also: Blue Ashva's ₹50 crore iD Fresh exit, creators' push for guaranteed fees, the fight over kirana orders and Meesho's ₹400 crore payments plan.

Description:

**GIVA raises over $100 Mn in a round led by Multiples**
Jewellery brand GIVA has raised over $100 Mn in a round led by Multiples Alternate Asset Management, with existing investors Epiq Capital and Edelweiss Discovery Fund taking part. Multiples gets a board seat. The money will go into GIVA's leadership team, manufacturing and offline expansion; its previous round was a ₹530 Cr Series C led by Creaegis in June 2025.
*Source: Entrackr, YourStory, VIE Stories*

**FMCG brands raise festive ad spends 10-15% despite margin and demand pressure**
dentsuX's Anil Solanki expects festive ad investment to rise 10-15% over last year, with some leading brands going as high as 20%. Nestlé India raised its advertising and marketing spend by 46% year on year and Marico by 44%, while Hindustan Unilever cut its ad spend. One executive said some brands move more than 50% of their annual inventory in the three festive months.
*Source: exchange4media*

**FirstClub crosses 3.5 million orders and enters beauty and personal care**
Quality-first quick commerce platform FirstClub has crossed 3.5 million orders and launched a beauty and personal care range of more than 80 brands across skincare, haircare, body care and personal care, priced between ₹300 and ₹3,500. It uses an ingredient-first approach and lists only products rated at least four stars. The category was among those earmarked for its $55 Mn Series B, led by Peak XV Partners and Sofina in June.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard*

**Blue Ashva exits iD Fresh Food in a ₹50 Cr secondary deal**
Blue Ashva Capital has fully exited iD Fresh Food in a secondary transaction of over ₹50 Cr for about 1% of the company, led by Upsurge India and co-led by Triumph Global Fund and White Whale Ventures. Investor demand exceeded the available allocation, and the valuation wasn't disclosed. iD Fresh's FY26 revenue crossed ₹870 Cr, up more than 25%, and the company is preparing for a potential listing.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard, Entrepreneur India*

**Amazon and Flipkart face a creator retention test as influencers seek guaranteed fees**
Established creators are pushing Amazon and Flipkart for fixed payments, minimum commitments and longer partnerships instead of commission-only deals. Executives quoted suggest a hybrid model of guaranteed content fees plus performance-linked commissions. Others say platforms must also offer creator discovery, monetisation tools and more ways to earn.
*Source: Storyboard18, Lapaas Voice*

**Meesho clears up to ₹400 Cr for its payments arm**
Meesho's board approved an investment of up to ₹400 Cr in its wholly owned subsidiary Meesho Payments, through a rights issue in one or more tranches. Meesho put ₹100 Cr into the unit in May 2026; the subsidiary posted a net loss of about ₹24.7 Cr in FY26.
*Source: Business Today, Investment Guru India*

**A new battle is taking shape over who controls the kirana's order book**
An Economic Times Prime analysis examines the emerging contest over who controls the orders kirana stores place with their suppliers.
*Source: Economic Times*
