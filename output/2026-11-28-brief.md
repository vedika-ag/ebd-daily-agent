🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **The Supreme Court has set a deadline for the new food labels.** FSSAI must notify front-of-pack labelling within **four months**, the timeline FSSAI itself proposed. Food companies then get **one year from notification** to comply, and that year counts as a voluntary implementation period. The labels need a separate red hexagon for each of high sugar, high saturated fat and high salt, and the font has to be "clear, prominent and readily legible". The court noted FSSAI's "reluctance" on the issue. *(ET Retail)*
- **Haleon is reportedly in talks to buy a controlling stake in HealthKart, at a $1.2–1.4 bn valuation.** The talks are at an early stage, according to Mint as cited by Inc42, and a full sale is also on the table. HealthKart is running an IPO in parallel and will pick whichever offer is better. It owns MuscleBlaze, HK Vitals and Gritzo and has 200+ stores in 90+ cities. Haleon (Sensodyne, Eno, Centrum) has not commented. *(Inc42)*
- **Swiggy's Instamart is cutting about 340 corporate and support roles.** The restructuring is aimed at removing overlap between teams and using more generative AI. Swiggy says it will keep investing in dark stores and frontline staff. In Q2 FY27, Instamart's gross order value rose 16.7% from the previous quarter to **₹9,230 Cr**, and its contribution margin improved to **-1% of GOV**. *(Entrackr, ET Retail)*
- **Foxtale nearly doubled its FY26 revenue to ₹382.6 Cr, from ₹198.7 Cr.** Its loss narrowed 10% to ₹66.1 Cr. Ad spend grew 68.5% to ₹178.3 Cr, which is 38% of its costs. It spends ₹1.22 for every rupee of revenue. Among peers, Minimalist made about ₹700 Cr in revenue with a ₹26 Cr profit, Innovist ₹686 Cr, and Sugar's revenue fell 11% to ₹361 Cr. *(Entrackr)*
- **ASCI says beauty and personal care is the worst category for influencer ad violations.** It flagged **456 BPC cases** in April–September 2026. The main problems were unproven acne, pigmentation and hairfall claims, exaggerated before-and-after visuals, and sustainability claims that lab tests contradicted. Overall, ASCI found 2,308 of 3,951 complaints in violation, and 97.93% of those were in digital media. 75% of advertisers didn't contest and fixed or pulled the ad, and 58% of those cases involved missing influencer disclosures. *(Inc42)*

The price band is ₹105–111. The issue closes 4 December and listing is due 9 December. FirstCry's stake sale has been halved to ₹150 Cr. *(Entrackr, YourStory)*
