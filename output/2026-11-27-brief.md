🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **The Supreme Court has ordered separate front-of-pack warnings for high fat, sugar and salt.** It directed the Centre and FSSAI to require a separate pictorial hexagon for each nutrient over the threshold, labelled "HIGH FAT", "HIGH SUGAR" or "HIGH SALT". This rejects FSSAI's plan for a single combined badge. FSSAI's revised proposal triggers a warning when any one nutrient exceeds ICMR-NIN 2024 thresholds, whatever the degree of processing, and adds a separate "CONTAIN NON CALORIC SWEETENER" label. Before the verdict, FSSAI had asked for about four months to finalise draft rules and proposed a 365-day voluntary period after notification. Separately, it is weighing rules for ecommerce and quick commerce that would require manufacturing date, expiry date and batch number on online listings. At least 20 D2C food brands have received FSSAI notices since May over sugar and "natural" claims. *(Inc42)*
- **GIVA has raised ₹90 Cr in debt from BlackSoil.** This is its second raise this month, after a $100 Mn+ round led by Multiples. The money will go to store capex and working capital. GIVA has 400+ stores in 80+ cities and sells D2C, on marketplaces and on quick commerce. FY26 revenue was about ₹865 Cr. In FY25, revenue grew 89% to ₹518 Cr and losses widened 22% to ₹72 Cr. *(Entrackr)*
- **ECCO India is targeting 50 stores by 2028, up from 14 now.** It is also aiming for 65% CAGR through 2030. Ecommerce (Myntra, Nykaa, Amazon, Tata CLiQ Luxury and its own site) is about 30% of the business, and ECCO says that could reach 50% "provided growth comes through full-price sales". It keeps prices the same online and offline, and sold through the festive season without discounting. It says delays in BIS licensing and renewals are limiting product availability. Its Jaipur and Ahmedabad stores are outselling some Delhi and Mumbai outlets. *(ET Retail)*
- **Urban Company is launching its own AC brand, Native.** Pre-bookings are open only on its app, with prices starting at ₹55,499. The ACs come with a five-year unconditional warranty that includes gas refills, plus a 10-year compressor warranty. Urban Company says the design is based on failure patterns from 1.8 Cr AC service jobs. It's another case of a services platform turning its own data into a private-label product. *(Inc42, ET Retail)*
