🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **RBI proposes a "Stop Switch" for digital payments.** Customers would be able to instantly block all outgoing debits, new payment mandates and new UPI app registrations from one account, while incoming credits and existing standing instructions keep working. RBI also proposes a voluntary "Trusted Person" who must approve high-value transfers within one hour. Feedback is open until **14 Dec 2026**, and the rules would take effect from **1 Jul 2027**. *(Inc42)*
- **Steadview Capital sold a 0.73% stake in Lenskart for ₹920.58 Cr in a block deal on Monday.** It sold 1.27 Cr shares at ₹725 each, a 2.6% discount to the previous close. Société Générale and Goldman Sachs were the buyers. Lenskart shares closed 4% lower at ₹714.50, which values the company at about ₹1.24 lakh Cr ($13 Bn). In Q2 FY27, Lenskart grew revenue 35.7% to ₹2,845 Cr and its net profit more than doubled to ₹232 Cr. *(Entrackr)*
- **Pee Safe's FY26 revenue grew 54% to ₹126 Cr, but its loss rose 80% to ₹7.2 Cr.** Advertising spend rose 76% to ₹46 Cr and is now about 34% of total costs. The company spends ₹1.06 for every rupee of revenue. *(Entrackr)*
- **Silver jewellery brand Zavya raised ₹22.6 Cr in a pre-Series A round** led by Daksham Growth Ventures, with Orios Venture Partners joining, at a post-money valuation of about ₹191.5 Cr. The brand is EBITDA profitable. It will put the money into D2C and quick commerce growth and open its first physical stores, as rising gold prices push younger buyers towards 925 sterling silver. *(Entrackr)*
- **EU Ambassador: the India–EU FTA is likely to be signed in December** and could take effect by mid-2027, after the European Parliament reviews it. Talks concluded at the January summit; EU ministers are now deciding whether to approve the signing. *(ET Retail)* *The signing date is the ambassador's expectation, not an official date.*
