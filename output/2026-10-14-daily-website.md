Title: Festive Ecommerce Sales Fall 10% to ₹30,000 Crore, Retail Inflation Hits 5.54% & Boldfit Raises ₹400 Crore

Excerpt: Festive ecommerce GMV fell 7-10% to about ₹30,000 crore in the first four days as pricier smartphones dragged demand, retail inflation rose to a near two-year high of 5.54% in September, and fitness D2C brand Boldfit raised over ₹400 crore from Bharat Value Fund.

Description:

**Festive ecommerce sales fall 10% to ₹30,000 crore in four days as pricier electronics drag demand**
Festive ecommerce GMV for October 7 to 10 was an estimated ₹29,800-30,900 crore, down 7-10% from ₹33,200 crore a year earlier, according to Datum Intelligence. Higher phone and laptop prices and the absence of last year's GST-cut boost hurt big-ticket buying, while fashion GMV grew 22-25%.
*Source: Moneycontrol, Business Today, Newsbytes*

**Online festive sales start slow as smartphone demand dips, quick commerce takes a bigger share**
Pricier smartphones, driven by component costs and memory chip shortages, are weighing on the opening days of the online festive season. Analysts expect mobile phones to make up about 30% of festive sales this year, down from 35% last year, while quick commerce is projected to take about 16% of festive sales, up from 12%. Amazon and Flipkart say demand for premium electronics is still growing.
*Source: Economic Times, Business Today, Whalesbook*

**Retail inflation rises to 5.54% in September, a near two-year high**
CPI inflation rose to 5.54% in September from 4.83% in August, the highest since October 2024. Food inflation climbed to 7.74% from 5.97%, rural inflation hit 6.08% and urban 4.88%, while core inflation edged up to 4.6%. The RBI, which recently raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50%, expects inflation to reach 6% in the October-December quarter.
*Source: ET Retail, Angel One, The Statesman*

**FSSAI may amend laws to make online food sellers more accountable**
FSSAI is considering rules requiring ecommerce and quick commerce listings to show manufacturing date, expiry date and batch number, and holding platforms answerable for misleading manufacturer claims, CEO Rajit Punhani said. It is also weighing mandatory disclosure of cloud kitchen images and locations and stricter checks on dark store storage. Authorities have already cited Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, Flipkart and Amazon India for issues including misbranding and invalid licences.
*Source: Economic Times, Business Standard, Inc42*

**Government denies imposing new 5% GST on ecommerce delivery services**
PIB Fact Check and CBIC have called a viral claim that the government newly imposed 5% GST on ecommerce delivery services wrong and factually incorrect. The 57th GST Council meeting had recommended cutting the rate on ecommerce delivery services from 18% to 5%, without input tax credit.
*Source: Economic Times, Outlook Money, LatestLY*

**Boldfit raises over ₹400 crore from Bharat Value Fund for retail expansion**
Bengaluru-based fitness and sportswear D2C brand Boldfit has raised over ₹400 crore from Bharat Value Fund, with existing investor Bessemer Venture Partners also participating. The company plans to use the funds to expand its retail footprint, scale manufacturing and pursue selective acquisitions.
*Source: Inc42, Business Today*

**Meesho shares rally 5% to a 10-month high on festive sale orders**
Meesho shares rose about 5% on October 12 to an intraday high of ₹245.45 on the BSE, in a weak market, after the company reported 75% year-on-year order growth on day one of its Mega Blockbuster Sale. Transacting customers rose 60% year-on-year. The stock is up 34% in 2026 and remains below its record high of ₹254.65 from December 2025.
*Source: Business Standard, Zee Business*

**Snapdeal Swagotsav sale records 59% growth in fashion units, buyers rise 63%**
Snapdeal's Swagotsav sale, which began on October 4, recorded 59% year-on-year growth in fashion units sold and 63% growth in fashion buyers. Non-metro customers accounted for 82% of orders, and active transacting fashion sellers rose 94%. Women's western wear and salwar suits each doubled in sales.
*Source: ET Retail, Storyboard18, MediaBrief*

**Corrugated box makers squeezed as kraft paper and freight costs rise**
Kraft paper prices have risen 22-25% in about three months, roughly ₹7-10 per kg, pushed up by costlier imported waste paper, higher freight and dollar swings. Kraft paper makes up about 75% of a box's cost, and makers say they have struggled to pass on increases; some units are discussing price hikes of up to 20%. The South India Corrugated Box Manufacturers Association warns the rise could reach 35%, and flags the gap between 18% GST on kraft paper and 5% on finished boxes.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, Corrugman, Whalesbook*

**Lenskart shares slump 5% amid bulk deal buzz**
Lenskart shares fell as much as 5% intraday on October 12 to ₹643.80 on the BSE and closed 4.4% lower at ₹647.95, after reports that early investor Alpha Wave Ventures was selling part of its stake. Block deals saw 2.93 crore shares worth ₹1,895 crore change hands at ₹647 apiece, though buyers and sellers have not been officially named. The stock is still up nearly 48% in 2026.
*Source: Inc42, Kotak Neo*

**Udaan targets IPO by early 2028 as turnaround takes hold**
B2B ecommerce platform Udaan expects to turn fully profitable by end-2027 and go public by end-2027 or the first quarter of 2028, according to CEO Vaibhav Gupta. It expects FY26 revenue to grow 10-12% with losses narrowing about 20%, after FY25 revenue fell 20% to ₹4,561 crore and net loss narrowed 37% to ₹1,055 crore. The company has cut its footprint from about 80 cities to roughly 16 clusters and focused on groceries and staples.
*Source: Business Standard, Shopifreaks*

**PharmEasy parent API Holdings cuts FY26 loss 67% to ₹520 crore**
API Holdings narrowed its consolidated net loss to about ₹520 crore in FY26 from ₹1,572 crore in FY25, while revenue rose about 13% to roughly ₹6,619 crore from ₹5,872 crore. The company credited a shift to higher-margin businesses such as diagnostics, generics and private labels, and tighter discounting in its B2R business.
*Source: Inc42, MediaNama*

**Doctor's Choice doubles revenue on protein demand, plans bigger production capacity**
Gurugram-based, bootstrapped protein food brand Doctor's Choice doubled revenue to ₹60 crore in FY26 from ₹30 crore in FY25, and expects to cross ₹100 crore in FY27. It is setting up a new manufacturing facility in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, and is targeting about 2,000 retail outlets by 2027.
*Source: ET Retail, Business of Food*

**India's direct selling industry reaches ₹23,522 crore in FY25, eyes ₹25,000 crore next fiscal**
India's direct selling industry grew at a 7% CAGR to ₹23,522 crore in FY25, according to a report by the Association of Direct Selling Entities of India and Kantar, and is expected to top ₹25,000 crore in FY26. The sector has about 91 lakh active direct sellers, 47% of them women, and six large companies control 68% of industry revenue. The report projects ₹31,095 crore by FY29 in its base case.
*Source: ET Retail, Indian Television, Trak.in*

**UPI merchant fee deferred until after Diwali**
NPCI has deferred the 0.4% MDR on person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000, originally due on October 15, to after Diwali. A new implementation date is yet to be notified.
*Source: The Week, TechCrunch, Outlook Business*

**FSSAI draft on ecommerce food rules expected**
FSSAI's proposed ecommerce food-labelling and platform accountability rules are expected to be published as a draft amendment for public comment before being finalised. No date has been announced.
*Source: Business Standard, Inc42*

**Diwali on November 8, second-round sales awaited**
Diwali falls on November 8. Amazon and Flipkart have not yet announced dates for second-round Diwali sales after their early October festive events.
*Source: Outlook Business, Sunday Guardian*
