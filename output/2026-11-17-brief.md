🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse**
- **D2C brands aren't planning price cuts, even with crude falling.** Inc42 reported on 16 November that the relief from cheaper oil will take time to reach procurement costs, and that smaller brands, without big players' buying scale, are taking a harder hit to operating margins. Its comparison: HUL faced 8–10% material inflation and raised prices only 2–5%, and Dabur raised 4% against 10% inflation. Brands are expected to cut grammage on small packs and raise prices on larger packs. *I could only read parts of the article.*
- **Handpickd's revenue tripled in FY26, but its loss nearly quadrupled.** The fresh-produce delivery startup's revenue from operations rose 3.3x to ₹28.6 Cr from ₹8.7 Cr in FY25, while its net loss widened to ₹43.1 Cr from ₹11.2 Cr, according to its Registrar of Companies filings reported by Entrackr. Procurement costs (₹28.9 Cr) roughly equalled product sales (₹28.6 Cr), which left almost no gross margin. Total spending rose 3.6x to ₹72.9 Cr.
