Title: Urban Company Q2 Revenue Jumps 41% to ₹537 Crore, AI Drives 70% of Meesho Orders & Shoppers Stop Returns to Profit

Excerpt: Urban Company's Q2 revenue rose 41% to ₹537 crore as its board approved a fundraise of up to ₹2,500 crore, close to 70% of Meesho's orders now come through its AI ranking system Prism, and Shoppers Stop returned to profit in Q2 FY27 with non-GAAP sales up 7% to ₹1,618 crore.

Description:

**Urban Company Q2 revenue rises 41% to ₹537 crore; board approves fundraise of up to ₹2,500 crore**
Urban Company's Q2 FY27 revenue rose 41% to ₹537 crore and net transaction value 39% to ₹1,432 crore, while net loss widened to ₹182.5 crore from ₹59.3 crore on ₹174 crore of spending on InstaHelp. Excluding InstaHelp, adjusted EBITDA rose 213% to about ₹30 crore. The board approved raising up to ₹2,500 crore, subject to shareholder approval.
*Source: ET Retail, Inc42, Free Press Journal*

**AI drives about 70% of orders on Meesho via personalised ranking**
Close to 70% of Meesho's orders now come through Prism, its personalised ranking system, which ranks products using signals such as location, browsing, clicks and ignored listings, said head of engineering Pratik Kumar. Its AI support agent, Chorus, handles about 60% of user queries.
*Source: Business Standard, Entrackr*

**Shoppers Stop turns profitable in Q2 FY27 as consolidated revenue rises 7% to ₹1,618 crore**
Shoppers Stop posted non-GAAP profit after tax of ₹6 crore in Q2 FY27 against a ₹7 crore loss a year earlier, with non-GAAP sales up 7% to ₹1,618 crore and EBITDA up 56% to ₹43 crore. On a GAAP basis, revenue was ₹1,366 crore and profit ₹3 crore. Premium products made up 73% of sales, beauty sales rose 18% to ₹367 crore, and the company expects to be debt-free by Q3 FY27.
*Source: ET Retail, afaqs!, MediaBrief*

**FSSAI issues notices to BigBasket, Flipkart and Gemini Edibles over misleading claims**
FSSAI has issued show-cause notices to BigBasket operator Innovative Retail Concepts, Flipkart India and Gemini Edibles & Fats India over ads for Freedom Cooking Rice Bran Oil. FSSAI says claims such as "reduces bad cholesterol" and "10,000+ PPM Natural Oryzanol" are not allowed under its 2018 advertising and claims regulations. Gemini was also flagged for selling on its own website without a central licence covering ecommerce.
*Source: Economic Times, Business Standard, Business Today*

**Snabbit matches Urban Company's InstaHelp in NTV, burns ₹32 crore less in Q2 FY27**
Home-services startup Snabbit recorded net transaction value of ₹72 crore in Q2 FY27, slightly ahead of Urban Company's InstaHelp at ₹71 crore. Its burn was ₹142 crore against InstaHelp's ₹174 crore adjusted EBITDA loss, and it processed 6.4 million orders, according to co-founder Ayush Agarwal.
*Source: Entrackr*

**Snabbit spent ₹16 to earn every rupee of operating revenue in FY26**
Snabbit's operating revenue rose to ₹12.25 crore in FY26 from about ₹26 lakh, while its net loss widened to ₹167.8 crore from ₹5.7 crore. Total expenses of ₹194.8 crore were led by service professional costs (₹63 crore), marketing (₹48.7 crore) and employee benefits (₹46 crore), working out to about ₹15.92 spent per rupee of operating revenue.
*Source: Inc42, Entrackr, Business Standard*

**FirstCry's core revenue engine is fighting too many battles**
FirstCry is expanding faster delivery while managing store costs, a slowdown at GlobalBees and a loss-making international business as quick-commerce competition intensifies. In Q1 FY27, revenue rose 13.1% to ₹2,106.2 crore and net loss narrowed to ₹44 crore; India multichannel grew 17.7% while GlobalBees revenue was largely flat.
*Source: Inc42*

**Festive marketing faces an ROI test as brands shift towards creators and targeted digital**
Brands are moving festive budgets toward creators and targeted digital and setting conversion targets at the briefing stage. Festive creator spend is estimated at ₹900 crore in 2026, up about 29%, across around 7,200 brands, according to Qoruz. Creators take an estimated 25-40% of festive digital budgets for ecommerce and D2C brands.
*Source: ET Retail, Buzzincontent, Marketing Mind*

**Plum targets over 40% annual growth, eyes ₹1,500 crore-plus scale as it expands distribution 7x**
D2C beauty brand Plum is targeting annual growth of over 40% and a scale of more than ₹1,500 crore, backed by a sevenfold expansion in distribution. Its FY26 revenue rose 28% to ₹515 crore, with modern trade accounting for 46% of sales.
*Source: ET Retail, Indian Retailer*

**Festive demand drives India's diesel sales in October**
State-run fuel retailers sold 3.65 million tonnes of diesel in the first half of October, up about 26% from the same period in September, as wholesalers stocked up ahead of Diwali and trucking picked up. State retailers have held pump prices since May and are estimated to be absorbing a loss of about ₹20 a litre, drawing customers from private players such as Reliance and Nayara.
*Source: Reuters, Business Recorder, Newsbytes*

**India's organised luggage makers to grow 5-7% in FY27 as input costs pressure margins: Crisil**
Crisil expects organised luggage makers' revenue to grow 5-7% in FY27, with rising input costs weighing on margins. Polypropylene, polycarbonate and polyamide, whose prices largely track crude, make up more than half the cost of making luggage.
*Source: ET Retail, Crisil Ratings*

**Amazon launches two new portable AI listening devices**
Amazon launched two Bee-branded devices that record and aggregate information through the day to provide reminders and AI-driven insights: the Frontier bracelet at $75, already on sale, and the Eclipse, a magnetic phone attachment priced at $100 and coming soon. They are the first devices under the Bee brand since Amazon acquired the startup last year.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, Reuters, BGR*

**Eternal and Swiggy report Q2 results on October 21 and 22**
Eternal, which owns Zomato and Blinkit, reports September-quarter results on October 21, and Swiggy on October 22.
*Source: Kalkine, Screener*

**Amazon's Great Indian Festival still live, Flipkart's next sale expected around October 23**
Amazon's Great Indian Festival, which opened on October 8, is still live with no end date announced. Flipkart's next Diwali sale is expected to restart around October 23; Flipkart has not announced dates.
*Source: Couponscrew, DesiDime*

**Caller name display from November 1, Diwali on November 8**
Telecom operators must display caller names by default on 4G and higher networks from November 1. Diwali falls on November 8, after which NPCI will notify a start date for the 0.4% UPI MDR on P2M payments above ₹2,000.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard*
