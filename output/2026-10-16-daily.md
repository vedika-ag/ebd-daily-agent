# EBD Daily — 16 October 2026

🛒 **Shopify to bring accelerated checkout service Shop Pay to India**
Shopify is preparing to launch Shop Pay, its accelerated checkout that saves shoppers' payment and shipping details, in India by the first quarter of 2027. It is in talks with payment orchestration platforms to integrate payment providers, and pricing is not yet confirmed. Shopify has not publicly confirmed the timeline; Shop Pay would compete with checkout players such as GoKwik, Shopflo, Razorpay and Shiprocket.
*Source: Inc42*

📞 **Centre directs telcos to display caller names from November 1**
The Department of Telecommunications has directed Airtel, Jio, Vodafone Idea and BSNL to roll out Calling Name Presentation (CNAP) from November 1, enabled by default on 4G and higher networks. Unsaved numbers will show the name in the caller's operator records. Businesses and bulk callers can display a preferred name only if it is a registered trademark or a trade name registered on the GST portal.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard, Business Today*

🍛 **Banaraswala raises $2 million from Prax Impex to expand across India**
Banarasi food brand Banaraswala, founded by Vibhanshu Mishra, raised $2 million in seed funding from Prax Impex. It plans to grow from three Delhi outlets to 50 company-owned and franchise outlets by March 2027 across Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi NCR and other Tier 2 markets. The funds will also go into menu development and franchise operations.
*Source: Entrackr, Indian Startup Times*

🥛 **Milky Mist launches high-protein milk to expand value-added dairy portfolio**
Erode-based Milky Mist has launched Milky Mist High Protein Milk in a 250 ml pack, initially in select trade channels, with wider distribution planned. It adds to the company's high-protein paneer, Greek yoghurt and Skyr range, which it says has seen demand rise about 50% a year over the past two years. The company is preparing a ₹2,035 crore IPO.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, Dairy Industry Expo*

💉 **Mankind Pharma to acquire 70% in Belgian biotech firm for €48 million**
Mankind Pharma will acquire a 70% stake in Belgium-based Armed Cell Therapeutics (ACTx) for €48 million (about ₹511 crore), paid in cash in instalments through its Dutch subsidiary. ACTx develops CAR-T cancer therapies, with lead programmes in non-Hodgkin lymphoma and small cell lung cancer. It is Mankind's first major move into experimental cancer research.
*Source: Business Standard, Upstox*

📊 **Flipkart Group FY26 revenue nears ₹1.5 lakh crore, loss narrows to ₹8,468 crore**
Flipkart Group's consolidated revenue from operations rose 18% to about ₹1.46 lakh crore in FY26, while its net loss narrowed to ₹8,468 crore from ₹8,646 crore. EBITDA loss fell to ₹5,891 crore from ₹7,504 crore, and Flipkart Ads revenue grew 31%.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard, Newsbytes*

📉 **Indian consumer firms face margin squeeze despite strong quarterly sales**
Analysts expect Indian consumer goods makers to report narrower September-quarter margins despite double-digit sales growth, as raw material costs outpace price hikes. Systematix expects operating margins at major staples firms to fall about 45 basis points year-on-year, including about 150 basis points at Hindustan Unilever and 137 at Britannia. Nestle India, the first to report, posted a wider operating margin of 23.8% against 21.8% a year earlier.
*Source: Reuters, Finimize, Gurutrade*

⚖️ **Delhi High Court stops FSSAI from acting against Emami over 'Healthy & Tasty' mark**
The Delhi High Court restrained FSSAI from coercive action against Emami Agrotech over its 'Healthy & Tasty' brand for edible oils, noting that FSSAI's notice did not explain why the name would mislead consumers. Emami had challenged a June show-cause notice and a July letter directing state food safety commissioners to act against it. The order is interim, and the matter will be heard again in February 2027.
*Source: Bar & Bench, Storyboard18*

🏦 **Flipkart's sales rise 18% and losses shrink as it eyes a public listing**
Flipkart's adjusted operating losses narrowed about 17% in FY26, and its adjusted operating margin improved 230 basis points to minus 5.4% from minus 7.7%. Net loss fell only about ₹180 crore because of higher finance costs, a write-off of deferred tax assets and one-off items. The group has moved its holding structure back to India ahead of a potential domestic listing; no IPO date has been set.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard*

👗 **Myntra swings to net loss of ₹579 crore in FY26, revenue drops 13%**
Myntra posted a net loss of ₹579 crore in FY26 against a ₹548 crore profit in FY25, including a ₹137.5 crore write-off of deferred tax assets. Operating revenue fell 13% to ₹5,242.5 crore on lower logistics income, while advertising revenue rose 35% to ₹1,236.4 crore. Excluding logistics and shipping, revenue from operations rose 12% to ₹4,123.3 crore.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard, Newsbytes*

💅 **Looksmaxxing blurs line between essential and discretionary for Gen Z shoppers: Myntra head**
Myntra head Sharon Pais said looksmaxxing is blurring the line between essential and discretionary spending for Gen Z shoppers. Gen Z makes up about half of Myntra's customers, and the platform's customer base has grown 30% so far in 2026.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard*

🍔 **Swiggy launches multi-restaurant ordering across 720-plus cities**
Swiggy now lets users order from up to five restaurants in a single checkout across more than 720 cities, with one payment and one tracking screen. Each restaurant prepares and delivers its order separately. Swiggy says 55% of users build two carts in a single session and one in five places a second order within 30 minutes.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard, Newsbytes*

📦 **Festive ecommerce orders up 31%, quick commerce up 85%: Unicommerce**
Ecommerce order volumes rose 31% year-on-year and GMV 17% in the first week of festive sales (October 7 to 13), based on Unicommerce's analysis of 33 million order items. Quick commerce orders grew 85%, marketplaces 26% and brand websites 27%. Average order value fell 10%, Tier 3 towns accounted for 42% of orders, and FMCG volumes rose more than 190%.
*Source: Business Standard, Indian Television, Free Press Journal*
