🛒 **Ecommerce & D2C Pulse** (week of 9 to 14 November; markets and news were quiet over the weekend)
- **Quick commerce orders surged over Diwali, and the newer platforms grew fastest.** Amazon Now's orders rose 18x, Flipkart Minutes' rose 4x and Swiggy Instamart's rose 162%, while Zepto sold 4.68 lakh diyas, according to an ET Now report from 10 November. A Business Today segment on 12 November put festive quick commerce order volumes up 75% year on year and GMV up 38%, led by Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. *These are platform-reported figures from single outlets, and the Business Today numbers don't name a data source.*
- **D2C brands took about a third of startup funding deals in October.** Per Entrackr, D2C startups raised around $200 Mn in October, nearly 30% of the $678 Mn raised by Indian startups that month. Boldfit's $42 Mn Series B was the largest D2C round.
