Title: ITC to Merge Yoga Bar Maker Sproutlife, Nuuk to Raise ₹100 Cr at ₹500 Cr Valuation & Zero-Sugar Drink Sales Double for PepsiCo and Coca-Cola

Excerpt: ITC's board approves merging Yoga Bar maker Sproutlife Foods into the parent, Nuuk is raising ₹100 crore from Wipro Consumer Care Ventures and RPSG at a ₹500 crore valuation, and zero-sugar drink sales double for PepsiCo and Coca-Cola in the September quarter, while ecommerce leads October's startup funding at $182.69 million.

Description:

**Yoga Bar maker Sproutlife Foods to merge with ITC; board approves scheme**
ITC's board approved on 2 November a scheme to merge its wholly owned subsidiary Sproutlife Foods, the maker of Yoga Bar, into ITC, effective from 1 April 2026, subject to approvals. No new ITC shares will be issued. Sproutlife became wholly owned on 28 September after ITC bought the remaining stake for about ₹645 crore, and it reported turnover of ₹451.71 crore last fiscal year.
*Source: ET Retail, Investywise, Multibagg, ThePrint*

**Nuuk to raise ₹100 Cr from Wipro Consumer Care Ventures and RPSG at ₹500 Cr valuation**
D2C home appliances brand Nuuk is raising about ₹100 crore (around $10.5 million) in a round led by Wipro Consumer Care Ventures and RPSG Capital Ventures, at a valuation of about ₹500 crore, sources told Inc42. Existing backer Khosla Ventures is also looking to invest. The money would go to R&D, international growth and its domestic supply chain; Nuuk closed a $6.6 million Series A in August 2025.
*Source: Inc42*

**Zero-sugar drinks hit the sweet spot as sales double for PepsiCo and Coca-Cola in Sept quarter**
Sales of zero-sugar drinks doubled for PepsiCo and Coca-Cola in the September quarter. At Varun Beverages, PepsiCo's Indian bottler, zero- and low-sugar products made up nearly 88% of the India sales mix in the quarter, up from 44% a year earlier, which it said helped protect gross margins despite higher raw material costs. Coca-Cola reported a 16% rise in global volumes for Coca-Cola Zero Sugar.
*Source: ET Retail, Whalesbook*

**Startup funding falls 47.5% to $678 Mn in October as ecommerce leads all sectors**
Indian startups raised about $678 million in October, down 47.5% from September, with no round of $100 million or more. Ecommerce startups led all sectors with $182.69 million across 27 deals, ahead of AI at $131.12 million across 22 deals.
*Source: Entrackr, Open*

**Reliance Retail's Q2 revenue rises 6.4% to ₹91,716 Cr as JioMart grows 42%**
Reliance Retail's gross revenue rose 6.4% to ₹91,716 crore in the September quarter, while JioMart's revenue grew 42% on higher order values and 24% more units per order. Retail EBITDA margin fell to 7.8% from 9.0%. Management said it is matching quick commerce rivals' prices in metros while prioritising unit economics over order volumes.
*Source: Storyboard18, BW Businessworld, Best Media Info*

**Milky Mist tightens storage SOPs across distribution network amid FSSAI scrutiny**
Milky Mist has introduced stricter storage, handling and temperature-control rules after an FSSAI advisory on perishable foods. The rules apply across quick commerce platforms, modern retail, distributors, carrying and forwarding agents, and depots. Paneer brought in ₹304 crore in revenue last quarter, and the company has separately denied receiving any FSSAI or Tamil Nadu notice over its paneer.
*Source: Moneycontrol, NewsBytes, Storyboard18*

**From warehouses to last-mile delivery: how India's logistics companies are handling the festive ecommerce rush**
Logistics firms are adding capacity as online festive sales grow: Datum forecasts online festive GMV of ₹1.50–1.55 lakh crore this year, up 25–29% from ₹1.2 lakh crore in 2025. Shadowfax entered FY27 with about 2.6 lakh delivery partners across 15,656 postal codes, and Ekart is expanding to 1,000 franchise outlets by end-2026. TeamLease expects temporary hiring across retail, ecommerce, quick commerce and logistics to rise 15–20% this festive season.
*Source: Mint, Allcargo Logistics, Inc42, Logistics Insider*

**Razorpay receives final IFSCA licence to expand cross-border money movement from GIFT City**
Razorpay has received its final Payment Service Provider licence from IFSCA, following in-principle approval in April 2026. The licence lets it move money into and out of India from GIFT City, with foreign-currency transfers, multi-currency accounts, international collections and cross-border settlements. RBL Bank and ICICI Bank are already integrated as banking partners.
*Source: Entrackr, Razorpay, Indian Startup News, Elets BFSI*

**Pernia's Pop-Up Shop narrows Q2 loss by 13% to ₹68.3 Cr, revenue up 31%**
Purple Style Labs, the parent of Pernia's Pop-Up Shop, narrowed its September-quarter net loss to ₹68.3 crore from ₹78.4 crore a year earlier, and from ₹88.1 crore in Q1. Operating revenue rose about 31% to ₹142.1 crore from ₹108.7 crore, while total expenses rose 36.9% to ₹211.2 crore.
*Source: Inc42, Multibagg*

**Solgram raises over $1 Mn in pre-seed round, targets 25% share of premium grocery market by 2030**
Gurugram-based premium grocery platform Solgram raised over $1 million in a pre-seed round led by DSG Consumer Partners and Saama Capital, with hospitality entrepreneur Kapil Chopra also investing, less than three months after launch. The money will go to cold-chain infrastructure, technology, sourcing and expansion across NCR before other Tier-I cities. Founded by Shashank Shekhar Sharma, it sells fresh produce, dairy, meat, seafood and other temperature-sensitive products.
*Source: ET Retail, Entrackr, VIE Stories*

**Reliance Consumer Products expands Independence biscuit portfolio with nine new variants**
Reliance Consumer Products has launched nine biscuit products across 28 SKUs under its Independence brand in Tamil Nadu, on sale from 2 November through general trade, modern retail, ecommerce, Reliance Retail and wholesale channels. The range includes Power Up, Milky Power, Butter Bliss and Marie Prime, priced from ₹5 trial packs and ₹10 sharing packs to ₹40–₹125 home packs. It has partnered with Ajith Kumar Racing to promote Power Up.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, DT Next, MediaNews4U*

**Protein consumption and LPG access expose India's rural-urban divide: SBI Research**
SBI Research finds Sikkim, Goa and Haryana spend the most on protein-rich foods per household, while Odisha, West Bengal and Chhattisgarh lag behind, with men showing higher protein diversity than women. Rural LPG access is in the low teens in states such as Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh, against urban penetration above 75%.
*Source: Fortune India, Exencial Research*

**Amazon's quick commerce bet: speed is not the key**
A Financial Express opinion piece argues that delivery speed alone won't decide Amazon's quick commerce push in India.
*Source: Financial Express*
