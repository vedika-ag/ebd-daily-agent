Title: Delhivery Crosses ₹3,000 Cr Quarterly Revenue With ₹41 Cr Profit, Amazon Doubles Its Creator Base & New-Age Beauty Brands Outpace Legacy Names

Excerpt: Delhivery's Q2 revenue rose 20% to ₹3,066 crore as it swung to a ₹41 crore profit, Amazon India's Influencer Program doubled to 2 lakh creators with creator-led shoppers up 92%, and new-age beauty brands such as Dot & Key, Minimalist and Innovist grew fast in FY26 while older D2C names slowed.

Description:

**Delhivery reports ₹3,066 Cr revenue and ₹41 Cr profit in Q2 FY27**
Delhivery's revenue from operations rose 20% year on year to ₹3,066.38 crore in the September quarter, crossing ₹3,000 crore for the first time. It posted a consolidated net profit of ₹40.6 crore against a loss of ₹50.5 crore a year earlier, and EBITDA nearly tripled to about ₹205 crore. It ended the quarter with ₹4,676 crore in cash.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42, Business Today*

**Amazon steps up creator investments to turn social media influence into sales**
Amazon India's Influencer Program has doubled to about 2 lakh creators, with about 75% outside metro cities. Amazon says 28 million shoppers bought through creator links between January and September 2026, up 92%, and earnings per creator rose about 60%. It is extending its affiliate programme to YouTube Shopping and Pinterest, and its Creator Connections tool lets brands post campaign briefs and budgets.
*Source: Economic Times Retail, Storyboard18, Lapaas Voice*

**New-age beauty brands enjoy revenue glow-up**
Newer beauty brands outgrew older D2C names in FY26: Dot & Key reached ₹799 crore, Minimalist rose 36% to ₹690.2 crore with ₹25.9 crore profit, and Innovist tripled to ₹686 crore. Plum grew 28% to ₹515 crore and Foxtale nearly doubled to ₹383 crore but lost ₹66 crore, while Sugar Cosmetics is estimated to have shrunk to ₹300–350 crore. Mamaearth parent Honasa's revenue rose to about ₹2,392 crore.
*Source: Economic Times Retail, Whalesbook, Indian Retailer*

**SEBI greenlights Atomberg's ₹450 Cr IPO**
SEBI has cleared Atomberg Technologies' IPO, which includes a fresh issue of up to ₹450 crore and an offer for sale of about 7.65 crore shares. Of the proceeds, ₹150 crore will go to brand awareness and marketing, ₹100 crore to R&D and ₹90 crore to repaying borrowings. Atomberg's FY26 revenue rose 34.8% to ₹1,293.77 crore, with a loss of ₹148.88 crore.
*Source: Inc42, Entrepreneur India, NewsBytes*

**Small online sellers can register for GST in other states using platform warehouses from November 1**
CBIC has notified changes to GST rules that let small ecommerce sellers, who pass on up to ₹2.5 lakh of input tax credit a month, register in another state using a platform's warehouse there as their principal place of business. Physical verification will happen only in the seller's home state, and about 9.5 lakh sellers are expected to benefit.
*Source: Free Press Journal, NewsBytes*

**Centre sets up National Social Security Board for gig and platform workers**
The Labour Ministry has constituted the National Social Security Board for Unorganised Workers and Gig and Platform Workers under the Code on Social Security, notified on October 30. Chaired by the Union Labour Minister, it includes representatives of governments, aggregators, unions and worker associations, and will recommend and monitor welfare schemes such as pension and insurance. The notification does not by itself start benefits; the government aims to cover 2.35 crore gig and platform workers by 2029–30.
*Source: Mint, Business Today, Angel One, PSU Watch*

**Decoding the commerce and lending engines of IPO-bound OfBusiness**
OfBusiness's FY26 consolidated profit rose about 21% to ₹724 crore while revenue fell about 7% to ₹20,645 crore. Its commerce business brought in ₹19,174 crore with EBITDA of ₹769 crore, a 4% margin, while lending arm Oxyzo's revenue rose about 23% to ₹1,489 crore. The company is expected to file draft IPO papers by November for a $600–800 million listing in March–April 2027.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard*

**Amazon India records sixfold rise in two-wheeler sales during the festive season so far**
Amazon India's two-wheeler sales during the Great Indian Festival 2026 ran at six times its average daily pace since the event began. Commuter motorcycles grew about 13.5 times, electric two-wheelers eight times and premium motorcycles 2.5 times. Non-metro locations, led by Tier II and III cities, made up over 60% of two-wheeler sales.
*Source: Autocar Professional*

**Global FMCG giants find an Indian answer to their Western slowdown**
With demand subdued in the US and Europe, companies including Unilever, Nestlé, Coca-Cola, L'Oréal, PepsiCo, Reckitt, Danone, Heineken and Pernod Ricard are leaning on India for growth. Premiumisation and quick commerce are the main levers, with Unilever and Coca-Cola deepening ties with ecommerce and quick commerce platforms.
*Source: Economic Times, SiliconIndia, Whalesbook*

**Bijnis rebrands as Zorit, raises ₹27 Cr for B2B quick commerce**
Zorit, formerly Bijnis, raised about ₹27 crore in a round led by Sparrow Capital, with Info Edge, WaterBridge Ventures and Better Capital taking part. It now runs an inventory-led model, stocking local dark stores and delivering to neighbourhood retailers in as little as 60 minutes, and says it has reached ₹120 crore in annualised sales. It reported a ₹44.04 crore loss in FY26.
*Source: Inc42, Indian Startup News*
