## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 💳 **NPCI defers 0.4% UPI merchant fee** — The planned Merchant Discount Rate on P2M UPI transactions above ₹2,000, due to kick in October 15, has been pushed beyond Diwali "keeping in mind the festive season." No new implementation date has been announced yet. This is direct relief for D2C sellers and smaller merchants relying on UPI during peak shopping days. *Source: The Week, The Federal*
- 🧾 **GST Council moves to ease seller compliance** — The Council (meeting Oct 8) recommended auto-sanctioning 90% of eligible refund claims on zero-rated supplies and inverted-duty cases on a provisional basis, and cutting the deficiency-memo timeline from 15 to 10 days. It's also considering letting small ecommerce sellers use platform warehouses for GST registration, which would ease a long-standing compliance pain point for marketplace sellers. *Source: Kashmir Observer*
- 🍱 **FSSAI tightens quick-commerce food rules** — FSSAI is moving toward stricter enforcement on expiry-date display and food labelling for quick-commerce platforms; ecommerce-related prosecutions have risen five- to six-fold over the past year as compliance lags platform growth. Relevant for F&B D2C brands selling via quick commerce. *Source: Inc42*
- ⚡ **Quick commerce is this festive season's battleground** — Quick-commerce sales are projected to surge ~110% YoY this festive season, capturing roughly 18% of all online festive shopping. Flipkart Minutes is now doing 1.1–1.2 million orders/day (up from ~390–400K in November) across ~1,200 micro-fulfillment centers in 150+ cities, nearly catching Swiggy Instamart; Blinkit still leads at 3.4–3.6M daily orders, Zepto at 2.4–2.6M. Amazon is expanding its "Amazon Now" quick-commerce service from ~15 to 300+ cities with 100 new urban fulfillment centers. *Source: TechCrunch*
- 💰 **D2C funding:** Fitness-gear D2C brand **Boldfit raised ₹400 crore** in a Series B led by Bharat Value Fund (13 Oct) — one of the larger D2C rounds this week. Smart-crib D2C brand **Cradlewise raised $12 Mn** to expand its product portfolio. *Source: StartupTalky, Inc42*
