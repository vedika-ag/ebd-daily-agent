## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 💳 **UPI merchant fee pushed past Diwali.** On 13 October, NPCI deferred the 0.4% MDR on person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000, which was due to start 15 October. No new date has been set. Reported exemptions include merchants receiving under ₹1 lakh a month via UPI, and the fee is reportedly capped at ₹300 per transaction. *Source: The Week, TechCrunch, Outlook Business*
- 🍱 **FSSAI weighs ecommerce-specific food rules.** The regulator is considering requiring quick-commerce and ecommerce listings to show manufacturing date, expiry date and batch number, and making platforms more responsible for misleading product claims. Cloud kitchens may have to disclose photos and addresses. These are proposals for now, with no draft or effective date yet. *Source: Inc42, StartupTalky*
- 💰 **Boldfit raises over ₹400 crore.** The Bengaluru fitness and sportswear D2C brand raised the round from Bharat Value Fund, with Bessemer Venture Partners also investing. It plans to spend on retail expansion, manufacturing and selective acquisitions. *Source: Business Today*
