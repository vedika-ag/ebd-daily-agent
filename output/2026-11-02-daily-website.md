Title: UPI Hits a Record 25.47 Billion Transactions in October, Millennials Lead AI Shopping in India at 84% & Retail E-Invoicing Pilot Likely From April 2027

Excerpt: UPI processed a record 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October as daily value crossed ₹1 lakh crore for the first time, McKinsey finds 84% of Indian millennials shop with AI tools, and GST authorities plan a voluntary B2C e-invoicing pilot from April 2027.

Description:

**UPI hits a record 25.47 billion transactions in October as festive spending lifts daily value past ₹1 lakh crore**
UPI processed a record 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October, according to NPCI data released on 1 November. Volume rose 5.8% and value 7.4% from September, and the average daily value crossed ₹1 lakh crore for the first time, with about 822 million transactions a day. Festive shopping during Navratri and Durga Puja drove the jump.
*Source: Entrackr, ET Retail, Outlook Money, NewsBytes*

**Millennials lead AI shopping adoption in India at 84%, ahead of Gen Z: McKinsey**
84% of Indian millennials use AI tools to search for products, compare options or make purchase decisions, ahead of Gen Z and Gen X at 74% each and boomers at 56%, according to McKinsey's Agentic Commerce Consumer Survey. India ranks second after China among 11 markets, with 76% of consumers using AI for shopping, and 87% of Indian AI users trust AI chat and search tools against a 73% global average. The survey of about 21,000 consumers was fielded in June 2026.
*Source: ET Retail, Storyboard18, Asianet Newsable*

**Retail e-invoicing pilot likely from April 2027 to improve GST compliance**
GST authorities plan a voluntary B2C e-invoicing pilot from 1 April 2027 in select sectors, recording invoices that businesses issue to consumers. Today, businesses report only aggregate retail sales in their GST returns. States will also get access to a B2C invoicing app on the GSTN portal, and restaurants are not part of the proposed pilot.
*Source: Business Standard, Free Press Journal, Business Today*

**New GST rules now let small online sellers register in other states through platform warehouses**
From 1 November, under the new Rule 14B, small sellers who sell only through ecommerce platforms can declare a platform's warehouse in another state as their principal place of business. They complete verification and Aadhaar authentication in their home state, and registration in other states needs the platform's consent. The route covers sellers within a ₹2.5 lakh monthly limit, though reports differ on whether that applies to input tax credit or output tax.
*Source: Mint, Free Press Journal, SetIndiaBiz*

**The shopping bots are coming for Amazon**
AI shopping agents that compare prices and pick products could challenge Amazon's role as shoppers' first stop and weaken its sponsored-listing ad business. Amazon has blocked Meta's Muse agent from buying on its platform, citing safety, while Walmart and Shopify are exploring ways to work with it. CEO Andy Jassy said few people shop through outside agents yet and that Amazon's own Alexa for Shopping should be the main agent on its store, though it may work with third-party agents over time.
*Source: Wall Street Journal, PYMNTS, CNBC, The Next Web*

**DigiDukaan connects over 21,000 retailers with more than 450 brands across three cities**
ONDC's DigiDukaan, which lets kiranas order from multiple brands, distributors and wholesalers through one digital interface, has connected over 21,000 retailers with 450+ brands across Hyderabad, Jaipur and Mumbai as of September, up from about 12,000 the previous quarter. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal chaired a Chintan Shivir with over 40 FMCG leaders in New Delhi on 30 October and urged them to scale it nationwide. Hyderabad accounts for more than 16,000 of the retailers.
*Source: DD News, Press Information Bureau, BW Businessworld, Asianet Newsable*

**Delhivery shares rally 9% after it swings back to profit in Q2**
Delhivery shares rose as much as 9.5% on Monday after it posted a ₹40.6 crore September-quarter profit, against a loss of about ₹50 crore a year earlier, on revenue of ₹3,066 crore. Jefferies kept its Buy rating but cut its target to ₹470 from ₹540 while raising its FY27 EPS estimate by 21%, and flagged Meesho insourcing logistics as a medium-term risk. Citi is also bullish, with a target of ₹545.
*Source: Moneycontrol, Business Standard, NewsBytes, Indian Opinion*

**Meesho's next monetisation engines: ads, Mall, content commerce and lending**
Meesho's advertising business is still at an early stage. Management credited the September quarter's margin gain mainly to logistics efficiencies rather than ad revenue, and Motilal Oswal expects ads to support margins from FY28. Meesho has not disclosed how much of its net merchandise value comes from Mall or content commerce, or set monetisation targets for either. It also facilitates credit for sellers through NBFC partners and earns a share of the interest.
*Source: Inc42*

**Decoding the commerce and lending engines of IPO-bound OfBusiness**
OfBusiness's FY26 consolidated profit rose about 21% to ₹724 crore while revenue fell about 7% to ₹20,645 crore. Its commerce business brought in ₹19,174 crore with EBITDA of ₹769 crore, a 4% margin, while lending arm Oxyzo's revenue rose about 23% to ₹1,489 crore. The company is expected to file draft IPO papers by November for a $600 to 800 million listing in March to April 2027.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard*

**Troovy raises ₹100 Cr in Series B led by Fireside Ventures**
Gurugram-based healthy snacks and kids' food brand Troovy raised ₹100 crore (about $10.5 million) in a Series B round led by existing investor Fireside Ventures, with Sharrp Ventures also investing. The money will go to R&D, manufacturing capacity, and distribution across quick commerce and offline retail.
*Source: YourStory, Inc42*

**upliance.ai in talks to raise up to $4 Mn for R&D and global expansion**
Smart kitchen appliance startup upliance.ai is in advanced talks to raise $3 million to $4 million in a Series A round, with existing backer Khosla Ventures set to reinvest alongside a new investor. The round is expected to close by January 2027. Khosla Ventures led its ₹34 crore seed round in February 2024 at a ₹143 crore valuation.
*Source: Inc42, Elets CIO*

**Skincare brand Numour in talks to raise $3.1 Mn led by Sharrp Ventures**
D2C skincare brand Numour is in talks to raise nearly $3.1 million (about ₹30 crore) in a round led by Sharrp Ventures, sources told Inc42. The deal has not closed yet.
*Source: Inc42*

**Beauty and personal care brand Hyeglo Beauty raises ₹7 Cr in funding led by Sauce**
Beauty and personal care brand Hyeglo Beauty has raised ₹7 crore in a round led by Sauce.
*Source: Economic Times*

**Licious rebrands its heat-and-eat range as Sizzl**
Licious has brought more than 80 heat-and-eat snacks, starters, sides and meals under a new brand, Sizzl, launched in Mumbai on 29 October. The company says the products are ready in 2 to 8 minutes and delivered in 30 to 45 minutes.
*Source: Restaurant India, Indian Retailer, MediaNews4U*

**Gen Z rewrites festive gifting with AI-assisted picks, artisanal hampers and self-gifting**
Gen Z shoppers are changing festive gifting, using AI to help pick gifts, choosing artisanal hampers and buying gifts for themselves.
*Source: ET Retail*
