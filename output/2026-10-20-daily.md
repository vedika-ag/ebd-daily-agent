# EBD Daily — 20 October 2026

🍔 **Flipkart is quietly building a food delivery challenger in India**
Flipkart has expanded an internal test of its food delivery service, Eat-in by Minutes, from a few hundred to more than 8,000 employees in Bengaluru, with plans to reach five or six cities by the end of the year. Ekart runs a dedicated rider fleet for it, and restaurants come from ONDC and direct sign-ups. The service sits in its own tab in the Flipkart app next to Minutes.
*Source: TechCrunch, Newsbytes*

🎆 **Beyond Diwali sales, Sivakasi eyes global fireworks market with exports**
Sivakasi, which accounts for about 85% of India's organised fireworks industry, is looking to tap overseas buyers as some shift away from China, home to about two-thirds of global fireworks output. Nearly 80% of its business, over ₹4,000 crore, comes from Diwali sales in India. Tamil Nadu announced an export-focused policy in June, including port logistics upgrades at Chennai and Thoothukudi.
*Source: Business Standard, News Today*

💎 **Sauce leads ₹10 crore seed round in lab-grown diamond jewellery brand So/Facet**
So/Facet, a lab-grown diamond jewellery brand founded in 2026 by former Unilever managers Aarushi Agrawal Naulakha and Abhishek Naulakha, raised ₹10 crore in a seed round led by Sauce, its first investment in the category. Mokobara's co-founders and the Chona family office also participated. The online store is due to open in November, with a target of more than 30 stores over three years.
*Source: Entrackr, Entrepreneur India, Indian Startup News*

✈️ **Cleartrip's losses widen to ₹805 crore in FY26; spends ₹897 crore on discounts and cashbacks**
Flipkart-owned Cleartrip's net loss widened 28% to ₹805 crore in FY26 as spending on cashbacks and discounts rose 47.5% to ₹897.5 crore. Operating revenue rose 20.8% to ₹198.3 crore while total expenses rose 21.9% to ₹1,045.9 crore. The company is targeting operational break-even by the end of FY27.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42, BW Disrupt*

🍜 **Zepto builds 'Seoul Street' in Delhi to test what fandom can do offline**
Zepto ran an invite-only Korean street experience in Delhi on September 30 with Nongshim as title sponsor and brands including Sprite, McCain Foods, Gimi Gimi and Cheffin. Zepto cited more than 4.6 lakh ramen searches and over 1 lakh Korean noodle searches on its platform. Chief brand officer Chandan Mendiratta called the approach "snowballing the fandom".
*Source: afaqs!, Storyboard18, EventFAQS*

🚗 **MakeMyTrip in talks to acquire Quick Ride through redBus**
MakeMyTrip is in talks to acquire car and bike pooling platform Quick Ride through its subsidiary redBus, in a cash-and-equity deal under due diligence, according to sources. redBus already runs commuter carpooling service rPool. Quick Ride's investors include Peak XV, Naspers and Venture Highway.
*Source: Entrackr*

🧴 **Deconstruct revenue grows 25x in three years to ₹165 crore in FY26**
Skincare brand Deconstruct's operating revenue rose 30% to ₹165 crore in FY26 from ₹127 crore, a 25-fold rise over three years. Total expenses grew 48.7% to ₹174 crore, and it swung to a net loss of ₹5.4 crore from a ₹10 crore profit in FY25.
*Source: Entrackr, Inc42*

🛵 **Curefoods pilots budget food delivery app MegaBite**
Cloud kitchen operator Curefoods is piloting MegaBite, a budget food delivery app live only in Bengaluru's HSR Layout, with dishes priced at ₹49-199 and no delivery or platform fees. It serves food from Curefoods brands including Krispy Kreme, Olio Pizza, Rolls On Wheels and Eatfit. Curefoods shut an earlier in-house delivery service in 2024 over high delivery costs.
*Source: Inc42*

⚖️ **FSSAI crackdown continues with fresh show-cause notices to Zepto and others**
FSSAI has asked Zepto for order logs, seller details, warehouse tracking, a root-cause analysis and proof of stock withdrawal and refunds after a customer received besan past its September 12 use-by date. Bakelite Food Processing was flagged over the labelling of a cream roll sold on Blinkit and Zepto, and supplement makers HealthyHey and BMS Lifescience over misleading claims.
*Source: Inc42, ThePrint, The Statesman*

💄 **K-beauty up 200%, men's skincare up 78%: Gen Z reshapes Myntra's beauty mix**
Myntra's "Beauty in the Age of Gen Z" report shows K-beauty orders up about 200% year-on-year and searches up more than 300%, with over 90 K-beauty brands now on the platform. Men's skincare orders grew 78% and hair mask purchases 110%. Gen Z accounts for about 60% of beauty sales, and half of Gen Z beauty buyers are from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
*Source: ET Retail, Social Samosa, Business Standard*

📦 **Festive supply chains go hyperlocal as brands move inventory closer to consumers**
Brands are shifting festive stock from central warehouses to city-level dark stores and fulfilment centres closer to shoppers to meet faster delivery. Quick-commerce orders rose 85% year-on-year in the first week of festive sales, according to Unicommerce.
*Source: ET Retail, Storyboard18, Business Standard*

📋 **Quick-commerce rules on expiry dates and refunds could start early next year**
Draft rules would require quick-commerce platforms to show manufacturing date, expiry date and batch number on listings and to give clear refund and complaint-redressal information. They could take effect from the start of the new year, after a draft notification and circulars are issued.
*Source: Zee Business*

🛍️ **Gen Z makes up 52% of Amazon's fashion and beauty customers in India**
Gen Z accounts for nearly 52% of Amazon's fashion and beauty customer base in India. Amazon also said the number of sellers crossing ₹1 crore in sales on the first day of its festive event rose 80% year-on-year.
*Source: Business Standard*
