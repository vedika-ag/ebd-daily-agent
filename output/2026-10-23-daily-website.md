Title: Swiggy's Q2 Loss Misses Estimates as Instamart Losses Widen, Simpler GST Registration for Small Ecommerce Sellers From November 1 & Shiprocket Enters Food Delivery

Excerpt: Swiggy's Q2 loss of ₹922 crore came in wider than estimates as Instamart's loss rose to ₹764 crore, small ecommerce sellers can register for GST in other states using platform warehouses from November 1, and Shiprocket launched direct food ordering for restaurants to cut aggregator costs.

Description:

**Swiggy posts bigger-than-expected quarterly loss**
Swiggy's Q2 FY27 net loss was ₹922 crore, wider than analysts' estimate of ₹792 crore, though narrower than ₹1,092 crore a year ago. Revenue rose 36% to ₹7,571 crore. Instamart's segment loss widened to ₹764 crore from ₹651 crore in the June quarter, on revenue of ₹1,306 crore, and its gross order value grew about 31%.
*Source: Reuters, YourStory, Upstox*

**GST registration for small ecommerce sellers to begin from November 1**
From November 1, sellers who sell goods only through ecommerce platforms can register for GST in other states by naming the platform's warehouse as their principal place of business, under a new optional Rule 14B. Eligible sellers must already be registered in a home state and have monthly input tax credit of no more than ₹2.5 lakh. These registrations can be used only for supplies made through ecommerce platforms.
*Source: Business Standard, India TV, Daijiworld*

**Swiggy to begin Instamart inventory model transition, eyes pivot within three quarters**
Swiggy plans to start moving Instamart to an inventory model from mid-Q3 FY27, completing the shift over two to three quarters, now that domestic ownership has risen above 58%. It expects the change to improve working capital by 8-10 days and add up to 80 basis points to Instamart's contribution margin once fully rolled out.
*Source: Inc42, Investing.com, MediaNama*

**Swiggy CEO Sriharsha Majety says rival Blinkit has the stronger flywheel, aims to fix basics before expanding**
Swiggy CEO Sriharsha Majety said Blinkit has built a stronger, compounding flywheel and a more resilient business, and that Swiggy will focus on a clear path to adjusted EBITDA breakeven rather than use its cash to open new fronts. An analyst on the earnings call pointed to a persistent gap of about 50% in net order value per store per day between Blinkit and Instamart.
*Source: ET Retail, Investing.com*

**Blinkit and Instamart chart different paths to profitable growth**
Blinkit posted adjusted EBITDA of about ₹184 crore in Q2 and plans to cross 3,500 stores by March 2027, while staying away from private labels. Instamart's loss widened to ₹764 crore as Swiggy prioritises breakeven over expansion and shifts it to an inventory model.
*Source: ET Retail, Business Standard, Inc42*

**Some pockets of stress emerge in India's FMCG demand ahead of Diwali**
Festive stocking by distributors is down by high single digits in parts of western and eastern India after recent price increases, with one eastern distributor reporting volumes 7-8% below last Diwali. Demand is growing in central and northern India, especially for packaged foods. In the west, rural markets are weakening after price hikes and crop damage while urban demand holds up.
*Source: Business Standard*

**Shiprocket enters food delivery with a direct-ordering model to cut restaurant costs**
Shiprocket has launched direct food ordering for restaurants through its fastrr platform, starting in Delhi-NCR with Vegorama Group's Punjabi Angithi and Bikkgane Biryani. Restaurants keep their own customer data and loyalty programmes while Shiprocket Quick handles delivery using riders from Ola, Porter, Borzo and Shadowfax. Shiprocket says aggregator commissions, taxes and discounts can take up to 40% of an order's value.
*Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, Newsbytes, StartupTalky*

**Flipkart sees three more VP exits, taking senior-level churn to 14 in over a year**
Flipkart vice presidents Kanchan Mishra (smartphones), Amit Sachan (product and technology) and Kamal Chopra (corporate tax) are leaving, taking senior exits to at least 14 in about a year. Reports link the churn partly to uncertainty over the company's IPO timeline. Rakesh Krishnan will take charge of the smartphone business.
*Source: Moneycontrol, Storyboard18, Newsbytes*

**Flipkart acquires minority stake in travel tech startup TravClan**
Flipkart has taken a minority stake in Delhi-based B2B travel platform TravClan, which serves more than 15,000 travel agents, to strengthen international holiday packages on Cleartrip and Flipkart Travel. Financial terms were not disclosed.
*Source: Inc42, YourStory, Entrackr*

**BlueStone slips into ₹4.4 crore loss in Q2, revenue jumps 45% year-on-year**
Jewellery brand BlueStone posted a consolidated net loss of ₹4.37 crore in Q2 FY27, narrower than ₹52.1 crore a year earlier, as revenue from operations rose 45% to ₹745 crore. It turned a standalone profit of ₹1.75 crore, and its board approved raising up to ₹300 crore through non-convertible debentures.
*Source: Inc42, Entrackr, Whalesbook*

**Chapter Home raises ₹63 crore from Bessemer, Stride Ventures and others**
Home and kitchen products startup Chapter Home, founded by former PharmEasy executives, raised ₹63 crore in equity and debt from Bessemer Venture Partners, Stride Ventures, Tanmay Bhat and family offices, at a valuation of close to ₹1,000 crore. The funds will go into new products and categories, technology, brand building and the first offline stores for its brand HOKIPO.
*Source: Entrackr, Business Standard*

**Neeman's plans to grow from 50 to 500 stores, eyes ₹1,000 crore business**
Footwear brand Neeman's plans to expand to 500 stores across India and reach ₹1,000 crore in revenue within about three years, up from ₹185 crore last year. It expects revenue of ₹350 crore by March 2027.
*Source: ET Retail, BestMediaInfo, Images Business of Fashion*

**Zepto sees 400% growth in festive wear orders during Navratri**
Zepto recorded a 400% rise in festive clothing orders during Navratri, alongside higher jewellery demand. Swiggy separately reported a 36.4% year-on-year increase in sweet orders between October 17 and 20.
*Source: ET Retail, Whalesbook*

**Quick commerce becomes a festive gifting channel, but brands are not equally stocked for it**
Quick commerce is becoming a festive gifting channel, but stock availability varies widely across brands on the platforms. Platforms lock in capacity and inventory early for festive peaks, and practitioners say dark stores often run out of bestselling packs by the first festive weekend.
*Source: ET Retail, Marketing Mind*

**Swiggy's Q2 report card, eMudhra to exit certificate business, Tonbo's founders pocket ₹137 crore**
eMudhra will exit its global SSL/TLS certificate business, about 1% of its revenue, after Google and Mozilla distrusted its certificates over weak private keys; its shares fell 20% on October 21. Tonbo Imaging's three co-founders made about ₹137 crore selling shares in the company's ₹852 crore IPO, which was fully an offer for sale and was subscribed 135.9 times.
*Source: Inc42, The Hans India*
