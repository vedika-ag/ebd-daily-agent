## 🛒 Ecommerce & D2C Pulse
- 🔍 **Delhi food safety drive finds lapses at quick-commerce warehouses.** On the Chief Minister's directions, inspectors checked warehouses and dark stores of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Zomato Hyperpure and Reliance Retail. They reported expired stock at Instamart and Hyperpure, poor cold-chain control and pests at a Zepto site, and unhygienic storage at Blinkit. Improvement notices were issued, and 19 of 59 desi ghee samples tested were found unsafe. *Source: The Statesman, The Tribune, Newsbytes*
- 💳 **D2C brands raise festive working-capital borrowing 15–20% to stock quick commerce.** Stocking quick-commerce micro-fulfilment centres across cities is tightening cash flow, pushing brands toward revenue-based lenders such as GetVantage and Recur Club. Recur Club has launched a ₹500 crore fund for 150–170 D2C brands, with an average ticket of about ₹3 crore. *Source: Whalesbook, Analytics Insight*
- 💰 **Startups raised $302.85 million last week.** 28 Indian startups raised $302.85 million in 12–17 October across 8 growth-stage and 21 early-stage deals, with Bengaluru leading at 19 deals. *Source: Entrackr*
