Title: Flipkart Sees 14 Senior Exits Amid ESOP and IPO Uncertainty, Myntra Slips to ₹582 Cr FY26 Loss & Netflix Credits Flipkart Bundle for Record India Subscriber Gains

Excerpt: Flipkart has seen 14 senior-level exits as ESOP liquidity worries and an uncertain IPO timeline weigh on staff, Myntra swung to a ₹582 crore loss in FY26 as revenue fell 14%, and Netflix logged its biggest-ever quarterly subscriber additions in India on the back of its Flipkart Plus bundle.

Description:

**Flipkart's summer of discontent: senior exits and ESOP worries cloud its IPO**
Flipkart has seen 14 senior-level exits, including Kanchan Mishra, Amit Sachan and Kamal Chopra, with three more vice-presidents set to leave. Some executives left because they saw no clear way to cash out their ESOPs, and former executives including Mukesh Bansal have written to Walmart's board seeking a buyback of vested options. The IPO has no firm timeline, with Walmart reportedly asking Flipkart to prioritise profitability while Flipkart Minutes needs heavy investment.
*Source: Inc42*

**Myntra slips into the red in FY26, reports ₹582 crore loss**
Myntra posted a net loss of ₹581.8 crore in FY26, against a net profit of ₹546.3 crore in FY25. Revenue from operations fell 14% to ₹5,242.5 crore from ₹6,063.8 crore, largely due to a sharp drop in logistics and shipping services revenue, while advertising and promotion spend rose 10.5% to ₹2,326.5 crore. Myntra attributed part of the loss to higher finance costs, the reversal of deferred tax assets and one-off exceptional items.
*Source: Inc42, MediaNama, Indian Retailer*

**Netflix credits Flipkart bundle and Indian shows for record Q3 subscriber additions**
Netflix posted its highest-ever quarterly paid net subscriber additions in India in July-September, crediting local shows and its first transaction-based loyalty bundle with Flipkart. Under the bundle, eligible Flipkart Plus members earn a 30-day Netflix Mobile subscription each month after four qualifying orders of ₹299 or more. India revenue rose about 18% year-on-year on a currency-neutral basis and viewing hours rose 24%; Netflix did not disclose subscriber numbers.
*Source: Inc42, Business Standard*

**Customer receives Lifebuoy soap instead of Realme phone; Delhi consumer commission holds Flipkart liable**
The District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (South II), Delhi, held Flipkart guilty of deficiency in service and unfair trade practice after a buyer who paid ₹10,574 for a Realme Narzo 50A received Lifebuoy soap bars in October 2021. Flipkart argued it was only a marketplace and that the seller could not process a return without product images. The commission rejected this, saying the platform has an "inherent responsibility" for customer support, including logistics, payment and complaint resolution.
*Source: LiveLaw, NewsX*

**Flipkart to launch "Flipkart for Earth", eyes premium sustainable products**
Flipkart has begun onboarding brands for a new sustainable and toxin-free offering across beauty and personal care, snacks and beverages, home decor, fashion and appliances. It is not yet clear whether it will run only on the marketplace or also on Flipkart Minutes. Flipkart has not made an official announcement.
*Source: Storyboard18, Economic Times*

**Lenskart and Meesho among top picks as value retail leads India's consumption story: Siddhartha Khemka**
Value-focused retail formats are growing ahead of premium and traditional formats heading into the festive season, with Lenskart and Meesho named among top picks by Motilal Oswal's Siddhartha Khemka. Motilal Oswal's store checks in Nashik found Lenskart the strongest performer, with top stores posting 20-25% like-for-like growth. Demand at value fashion stores was healthy through August but softened in September and October as the festive calendar shifted and Pitrupaksha weighed on footfalls.
*Source: Economic Times, Investment Guru India*

**Startup funding falls 22% week on week as D2C menswear brand The Pant Project raises ₹109 crore**
Indian startups raised about $184 million across 17 deals from October 19 to 23, led by AI and healthtech, per Inc42; Entrackr counts $133.9 million across 15 deals. The Pant Project's Series B, led by Mirae Asset Venture Investments, will fund growth from 25 stores to more than 150 in two to three years and a move into jeans, athleisure and casualwear.
*Source: Inc42, Entrackr, DealStreetAsia, Indian Retailer*

**L'Oréal steps up its quick commerce push in India**
L'Oréal said its India business is gaining momentum and that it is expanding its skincare range and online presence, with a focus on quick commerce. Its dermatology brand CeraVe is expanding into new cities.
*Source: Business Standard, Storyboard18*

**Flipkart Minutes teams up with Ramsay Brothers for a 10-minute Halloween miniseries**
Flipkart Minutes and McCann India have launched "The Last Delivery", a three-part horror miniseries made with the Ramsay Brothers, with each episode running 10 minutes to fill the wait for a delivery. Customers can watch it on the Play screen after placing an order from October 25. It was directed by Deepak Tulsi Ramsay and written by Gopal Ramsay.
*Source: ET Retail, Mediabrief, Indian Television, Campaign Brief Asia*

**Puresta tackles the skincare paradox with clinical trust and AI**
Skincare startup Puresta, founded by former The Man Company leaders, is using clinical credibility, trial kits, AI skin analysis and content experiments to win customers as digital ad costs rise. It owns dermatologist-formulated D2C brand SKINQ and launched EVERQ in September, a platform that links an AI skin assessment with dermatologist consultations and personalised treatment. Puresta raised ₹34 crore in a pre-seed round led by Spring Marketing Capital.
*Source: Storyboard18, Best Media Info, Marketech APAC*
