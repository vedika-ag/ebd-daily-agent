# EBD Daily — 19 October 2026

🔍 **Delhi food safety drive finds lapses at quick-commerce warehouses**
On the Delhi Chief Minister's directions, food safety officials inspected warehouses and dark stores of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Zomato Hyperpure and Reliance Retail. They reported expired stock at Instamart and Hyperpure, poor cold-storage control and pests at a Zepto site, and unhygienic storage at Blinkit. Improvement notices were issued, and 19 of 59 desi ghee samples tested were found unsafe.
*Source: The Statesman, The Tribune, Newsbytes*

💰 **Indian startups raised $302.85 million last week**
28 Indian startups raised $302.85 million between October 12 and 17, across 8 growth-stage and 21 early-stage deals. Bengaluru led with 19 deals, followed by Hyderabad with 3 and Delhi-NCR with 2.
*Source: Entrackr*

🚚 **Ekart FY26 loss narrows by 98% to ₹26 crore despite a fall in top line**
Instakart Services, the Flipkart-owned company that runs Ekart, cut its FY26 loss to ₹26 crore from ₹1,508 crore as total expenses fell 13% to ₹13,638 crore. Revenue from customer contracts fell 4.2% to ₹13,194 crore: logistics and shipping revenue dropped 39.5% to ₹6,419 crore while warehousing revenue rose 44% to ₹2,212 crore. Flipkart contributed about ₹9,112 crore, or 69% of operating revenue.
*Source: Inc42, Lapaas Voice*

🏷️ **FSSAI issues notices to Nestle India and HUL over misleading product claims**
FSSAI issued show-cause notices to Nestle India over Nescafé ready-to-drink products sold online as "Flavoured Milk" on the front but declared "Proprietary Food" on the back, and over Resource Renal Vanilla powder sold without required approval. HUL was flagged for Horlicks Women's Plus claims such as "Healthy Birth Weight" and "Improves Bone Health". The notices cite FSSAI's 2018 advertising and claims and 2020 labelling regulations.
*Source: Mint, Business Standard, Business Today*

🍽️ **Noida cloud kitchen operator says Swiggy offered up to 70% discounts without consent on 25 outlets**
A Noida-based cloud kitchen operator has alleged that Swiggy ran discounts of up to 70% on 25 of its outlets without its consent. Restaurant partners have previously complained of ad charges deducted without consent; Swiggy has said such cases affect fewer than 1% of partners and are reversed where there is no consent trail.
*Source: Moneycontrol, MediaNama*

🍜 **Burma Burma parent Hunger Pangs files DRHP for ₹150 crore fresh issue**
Hunger Pangs, which runs the Burma Burma restaurant chain, has filed draft IPO papers for a ₹150 crore fresh issue plus an offer for sale of about 2.79 crore shares. About ₹117.3 crore will fund 27 new company-owned outlets over FY28 and FY29. Revenue from operations rose 46.6% to ₹155.5 crore in FY26.
*Source: Entrackr, Upstox, Entrepreneur India*

👖 **The Pant Project raises ₹109 crore to expand store network and portfolio**
Mumbai-based menswear brand The Pant Project raised ₹109 crore in a Series B round led by Mirae Asset Venture Investments, with existing investor Sorin Investments participating, plus venture debt from Alteria Capital. It plans to grow from 25 to about 150 stores over the next two to three years and add jeans, athleisure and casualwear.
*Source: Inc42, Whalesbook*

🚫 **Maharashtra FDA suspends licences of Reliance, Blinkit and Zomato Hyperpure warehouses**
Maharashtra's FDA suspended licences of five warehouses run by Reliance Consumer Products, Reliance Retail, Blinkit and Zomato Hyperpure, seized 8,392 kg of stock worth ₹48.08 lakh and shut two unlicensed units. It is investigating whether expired and rejected food meant for destruction was sent back into the market. At a Hyperpure warehouse serving Blinkit, inspectors found 5,431 expired or damaged products worth ₹7.80 lakh.
*Source: Economic Times, Newsbytes, Indian Television*

🔬 **India's R&D Achilles' heel needs a $100 billion fix: Peak XV Partners MD**
Peak XV Partners MD Rajan Anandan said India spends about $18 billion a year on R&D, roughly 0.65% of GDP, and needs to raise that to at least $100 billion. He compared this with the US at 3.5% of GDP and China at 2.5%.
*Source: Business Standard*

💳 **Quick commerce and uncertain demand push up festive borrowing for consumer brands**
D2C and consumer brands are raising working-capital borrowing by 15-20% this festive season as stocking quick-commerce micro-fulfilment centres across cities ties up cash. Many are turning to revenue-based lenders such as GetVantage and Recur Club, which has launched a ₹500 crore fund for 150-170 D2C brands with an average ticket of about ₹3 crore.
*Source: Mint, Whalesbook, Analytics Insight*

👕 **Uniqlo India net profit rises 41% to ₹253 crore in FY26; company bets on expansion**
Uniqlo India's revenue rose 44% to ₹1,724 crore in FY26 and net profit rose 41% to ₹253 crore. Parent Fast Retailing has named India among the regions drawing shoppers as it expands.
*Source: ET Retail, Whalesbook*

📉 **FMCG firms grapple with input cost inflation in Q2, brace for margin pressure in H2**
FMCG companies expect margin pressure in the second half of FY27 as raw material costs rise, with palm oil about 21% higher year-on-year in rupee terms in September and sugar about 18% higher. Many absorbed part of the increase to protect festive demand and are weighing price hikes after Diwali.
*Source: ET Retail, Lapaas Voice*
