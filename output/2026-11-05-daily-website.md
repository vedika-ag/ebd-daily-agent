Title: Nykaa's Q2 Profit Triples to ₹101 Cr, Lenskart's Profit Jumps 125% & Amazon Now Crosses $1 Bn in Annualised Sales

Excerpt: Nykaa's Q2 profit triples to ₹101 crore on 28% revenue growth, Lenskart's profit jumps 125% to ₹232 crore, and Amazon Now crosses $1 billion in annualised sales as it adds Amritsar and Jaipur. Also: Razorpay's reported IPO approval, Flipkart Minutes closing in on Blinkit's order values, Nykaa Now's expansion, big FMCG firms gaining share, Eggoz's FY26 numbers and how Bharat Z shops.

Description:

**Nykaa's Q2 profit more than triples to ₹101 Cr as revenue rises 28%**
Nykaa's revenue from operations rose 28% year on year to ₹3,006 Cr in Q2 FY27, and consolidated net profit rose about 207% to ₹101 Cr. GMV grew 30% to ₹6,151 Cr, and EBITDA rose about 66% to ₹263 Cr, with the margin widening to 8.75% from 6.75%. Beauty net sales rose 28% to ₹2,536 Cr, and the fashion business was EBITDA-positive for a third straight quarter.
*Source: Inc42, Upstox, YourStory, Kotak Neo*

**Lenskart's Q2 profit jumps 125% to ₹232 Cr on 36% revenue growth**
Lenskart's consolidated profit after tax rose about 125% to ₹232.45 Cr in Q2 FY27 from ₹103.45 Cr, while revenue from operations rose about 36% from ₹2,096.14 Cr to about ₹2,845 Cr. EBITDA (pre-Ind AS 116) grew 65% to ₹392 Cr. Profit was up 1.8% from ₹228.4 Cr in Q1 FY27.
*Source: Inc42, YourStory, Angel One*

**Amazon takes its quick delivery service to Amritsar and Jaipur**
Amazon has added Amritsar and Jaipur to Amazon Now, which now covers more than 60 cities and towns, including smaller markets such as Gurdaspur, Jalandhar, Tirupati, Guntur and Warangal. Amazon's Srikant Sree Ram said the service has crossed $1 Bn in annualised gross sales in India, with orders doubling every quarter since launch. Amazon is scaling Amazon Now to 100 cities with more than 1,000 micro-fulfilment centres.
*Source: Indian Retailer, Franchise India*

**Razorpay reportedly gets SEBI approval for a ₹5,000 to 6,000 Cr IPO**
Razorpay, which filed its draft papers confidentially in June, has reportedly received SEBI's approval for its IPO, according to media reports citing sources. It is targeting a valuation of about $5 Bn, below its 2021 round, with a listing likely between January and March 2027. It must next publish an updated draft for 21 days of public comments; no price band or dates have been set.
*Source: Inc42, The Hans India, Indian Startup News*

**Flipkart Minutes nears Blinkit on order values, helped by electronics**
A UBS report estimates Flipkart Minutes' net order value at about ₹500 to ₹530 excluding mobile phones, close to Blinkit's ₹518 in Q1 FY27. With phones included, Minutes' order value is estimated to be higher than Blinkit's, reflecting Flipkart's strength in mobiles and electronics. Flipkart has not disclosed order volumes or margins for Minutes.
*Source: Indian Retailer, NewsBytes, Open*

**Nykaa pushes quick commerce expansion with Nykaa Now**
Nykaa Now is live in 15 cities, and Nykaa plans to take it to more than 25 by the end of FY27. Nykaa is offering 30 to 60 minute delivery rather than 10 minutes, so it can run fewer rapid stores with a deeper range: over 1,000 brands and more than twice the beauty and personal care SKUs of quick commerce platforms. It says Nykaa Now customers buy 15% more often than those on its main platform.
*Source: Mint, D2C Insider, GuruFocus*

**Big FMCG companies outgrow the market as smaller rivals get squeezed**
Listed FMCG leaders are taking share from regional and unorganised players, as higher costs and slowing rural demand squeeze smaller companies. FMCG volume growth cooled to 3.6% in the July to September quarter, according to Whalesbook, while retail inflation reached 5.54% in September and food inflation 7.74%. Larger companies are helped by deeper distribution networks and greater buying power.
*Source: ET Retail, Whalesbook*

**Eggoz's FY26 revenue grows 39% to ₹180 Cr as losses widen to ₹42 Cr**
Egg brand Eggoz's revenue from operations rose 39% to about ₹180 Cr in FY26, while its net loss rose 83% to about ₹42 Cr from ₹23 Cr. Employee and advertising costs grew faster than revenue, with employee benefit expenses up 50% to ₹30.8 Cr. EBITDA loss was ₹44 Cr, a margin of -24.44%.
*Source: Entrackr*

**Bharat Z discovers brands through algorithms but buys on family advice: MSL India-YKA study**
In MSL India and Youth Ki Awaaz's survey of 3,509 people under 30 in Tier 2, 3 and 4 cities, 55.8% said they first hear about products they later buy through Instagram Reels, posts or creators. But 73.1% trust family and close friends most before buying something new, and 54.8% trust Reels and short-video ads when considering a purchase. "This generation discovers through algorithms but decides through relationships," said Amit Misra, CEO of MSL South Asia.
*Source: afaqs!, Social Samosa, Medianews4u*
