Title: Razorpay Gets SEBI Nod for ₹6,000 Cr IPO, Urban Company Shareholders Approve ₹2,500 Cr Raise & Eternal May Push Back Its 100% EV Fleet Target

Description:

**Razorpay gets SEBI nod for an IPO of up to ₹6,000 crore**
Razorpay has received SEBI's approval for an IPO that could raise ₹5,000 crore to ₹6,000 crore, with listing expected between January and March 2027. The company filed confidentially in June, targeting a valuation of $5 billion to $6 billion. Separately, the Karnataka High Court rejected a plea by senior executives to quash SFIO proceedings over alleged KYC lapses.
*Source: Reuters, Business Today, BW Disrupt, CIOL*

**Urban Company shareholders approve a ₹2,500 crore fundraise**
Urban Company's shareholders approved raising up to ₹2,500 crore through a qualified institutional placement, with 99.29% voting in favour by postal ballot. The board cleared the plan with Q2 FY27 results, when its net loss widened to ₹182.48 crore from ₹59.33 crore as revenue rose to ₹537.29 crore. It invested about ₹174 crore in its InstaHelp house help service in the quarter.
*Source: ET Retail, NewsBytes, Lapaas Voice*

**Zomato and Blinkit parent Eternal may push back its 100% EV delivery fleet target**
Eternal is reviewing its goal of an all-electric delivery fleet by 2030 because rapid expansion and uneven access to EVs have made the switch harder. Zomato had about 1.5 lakh monthly active riders when the target was set; Zomato and Blinkit together now have about 12 lakh. EVs make up 12% to 13% of the fleet, and Chief Sustainability Officer Anjalli Ravi Kumar said a new date is being worked on but did not commit to 2035.
*Source: Economic Times, Adgully, NewsBytes*

**Consumer spending on ITC's Savlon and Nimyle expected to double to over ₹2,000 crore**
Consumer spending on Savlon and Nimyle has crossed ₹1,000 crore and is growing over 20% a year, and is expected to more than double to over ₹2,000 crore in the medium term. ITC bought Savlon from Johnson & Johnson in 2015 and has grown it nearly ninefold; Nimyle, bought in 2018, has grown about eightfold.
*Source: Economic Times, Business Standard*

**Quick commerce and ecommerce are converging, says WPP Media's Sairam Ranganathan**
WPP Media's Sairam Ranganathan said quick commerce is taking a growing share of online sales, pushing ecommerce platforms to rethink strategy as shoppers prioritise speed and convenience. He said quick commerce made up about 20% of one WPP client's sales four years ago, and that speed alone doesn't decide which channel shoppers choose.
*Source: Storyboard18*

**Indian D2C brands bet big on connected TV beyond performance marketing**
D2C brands are adding connected TV to their performance marketing to build scale, salience and trust. Hair care brand Traya said layering CTV on top of performance marketing revived stalled markets and shortened timelines. Agency executives said TV is meant to make a brand bigger, not to replace performance ads.
*Source: afaqs!*

**Havas Media Network India wins integrated media mandate for Milky Mist's Capella**
Havas Media Network India will handle media and performance strategy for Capella, Milky Mist's luxury chocolate line. The account will be run by an integrated team from Havas Media India and its digital agency PivotRoots.
*Source: afaqs!, Exchange4media, Storyboard18*

**Startup funding falls to just over $25 million in the second week of November**
Indian startups raised just over $25 million between 9 and 13 November, according to Inc42, down from $255 million the week before. Clean tech led with three deals worth $16.2 million. D2C deals included QB Men and KL Rahul-backed PlayBlue, both undisclosed.
*Source: Inc42*

**Delhi founder whose ramen business went from ₹25,000 daily sales to zero seeks advice online**
A 23-year-old Delhi entrepreneur started a ramen business in a rented room in August 2022 and reached ₹25,000 in daily sales within three months. Sales then fell to ₹10,000 to ₹12,000 a day and eventually to zero, debt mounted, and the business shut in late 2023. The founder, who blamed overspending, now runs a water fountain business and asked a Reddit community for advice on scaling it.
*Source: Moneycontrol, Alo Japan*

**D2C brands rethink pricing**
D2C brands are rethinking how they price their products, according to Economic Times' Morning Dispatch.
*Source: Economic Times*
